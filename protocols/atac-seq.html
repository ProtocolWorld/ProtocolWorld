ATAC-seq

About 3 h hands-on · 2026-11-26

Omni-ATAC-seq library preparation from 50,000 cells: nuclei lysis with digitonin/Tween-20, Tn5 tagmentation, qPCR-guided amplification, cleanup, QC and sequencing guidance for mapping open chromatin.

⬇ Word (.docx)⬇ PowerPoint (.pptx)

Purpose

To map open chromatin genome-wide from about 50,000 cells by tagging accessible DNA with a hyperactive Tn5 transposase and sequencing the fragments, using the Omni-ATAC detergent improvements to lower mitochondrial and background reads.

Principle

Tn5 loaded with sequencing adapters cuts and tags DNA preferentially where chromatin is accessible in intact nuclei. The tagged fragments are purified, amplified with indexed primers and sequenced; nucleosome-free fragments (below about 150 bp) mark open regions, while longer fragments span one or more nucleosomes and give a laddered size profile [1][2][3]. Omni-ATAC adds digitonin, Tween-20 and NP-40 to cut mitochondrial reads and improve signal, and it also works on frozen cells and tissue [1]. A qPCR side reaction sets the number of extra PCR cycles so libraries are not over-amplified [2][4].

Materials and reagents

ItemDetails
Cells50,000 viable cells per reaction (a window of about 25,000–75,000 works); do not fix them [2]. Cultured cells may be pretreated with 200 U/mL DNase for 30 min at 37 °C to remove free DNA [1]
Resuspension buffer (RSB)10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.4, 10 mM NaCl, 3 mM MgCl₂; stable at room temperature [1][4]
Omni lysis buffer (50 µL per sample)RSB with 0.1% NP-40, 0.1% Tween-20 and 0.01% digitonin (48.5 µL RSB + 0.5 µL 10% NP-40 + 0.5 µL 10% Tween-20 + 0.5 µL 1% digitonin) [1][4]
Wash bufferRSB with 0.1% Tween-20 only [1][4]
Transposition mix (50 µL)25 µL 2× TD buffer, 2.5 µL Tn5 (TDE1), 16.5 µL PBS, 0.5 µL 1% digitonin, 0.5 µL 10% Tween-20, 5 µL nuclease-free water [1][4]
PCR mix (50 µL)10 µL transposed DNA, 10 µL water, 2.5 µL of 25 µM Ad1_noMX primer, 2.5 µL of 25 µM barcoded Ad2.x primer, 25 µL NEBNext High-Fidelity 2× PCR Master Mix [2][4]
qPCR side reaction (15 µL)5 µL pre-amplified DNA, 4.41 µL water, 0.25 µL of each 25 µM primer, 0.09 µL 100× SYBR Green I, 5 µL NEBNext 2× mix [2]
DNA cleanupQiagen MinElute or Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrator-5 columns, eluting in 10 µL; AMPure XP SPRI beads for library cleanup [1][2][4][5]
Library QC reagentsAgilent High Sensitivity DNA chip, Qubit dsDNA HS and, preferably, KAPA Library Quant [2][4]

Equipment

  • Refrigerated centrifuge (500 × g, 4 °C)
  • Thermomixer set to 37 °C and 1,000 rpm
  • Thermal cycler and a qPCR instrument
  • Magnetic stand for SPRI beads
  • Bioanalyzer or TapeStation
  • Low-bind 0.2 mL and 1.5 mL tubes [5]
  • Cell counter or hemocytometer

Procedure

  1. Count viable cells and aliquot 50,000. Optionally treat cultured cells with DNase (200 U/mL, 37 °C, 30 min) and wash in cold PBS [1]. (45 min)
  2. Pellet the cells at 500 × g for 5 min at 4 °C and remove all supernatant [1][4]. (10 min)
  3. Resuspend the pellet in 50 µL cold Omni lysis buffer by pipetting up and down 3 times. Incubate on ice for 3 min [1][4]. (5 min)
  4. Add 1 mL cold wash buffer and invert the tube 3 times. Spin at 500 × g for 10 min at 4 °C and remove the supernatant [1][4]. (15 min)
  5. Resuspend the nuclei in 50 µL transposition mix by pipetting 6 times. Incubate at 37 °C for 30 min at 1,000 rpm [1][4]. (30 min)
  6. Clean up immediately with a MinElute or Zymo-5 column and elute in 10 µL. Fragments will not amplify once the DNA has melted, so do not delay [1][2][4]. (15 min)
  7. Set up the 50 µL PCR. Run 72 °C for 5 min, 98 °C for 30 s, then 5 cycles of 98 °C for 10 s, 63 °C for 30 s and 72 °C for 1 min [2][4]. (25 min)
  8. Take 5 µL of the pre-amplified product into the 15 µL qPCR reaction. Run 98 °C for 30 s, then 20 cycles of 98 °C for 10 s, 63 °C for 30 s and 72 °C for 1 min [2][4]. (45 min)
  9. Plot linear fluorescence against cycle. Pick the cycle at one-third of maximum fluorescence; this is N, the number of extra cycles for the remaining 45 µL [2]. (10 min)
  10. Run the remaining 45 µL for 98 °C for 30 s, then N cycles of 98 °C for 10 s, 63 °C for 30 s and 72 °C for 1 min. Stop before saturation to limit bias [2][4]. (20–30 min)
  11. Clean up with AMPure XP beads at 1.6–1.8× (for example 81 µL beads on 45 µL PCR), wash twice with 80% ethanol and elute in 20 µL [4][5]. (30 min)
  12. Check size distribution on a High Sensitivity DNA chip (1 µL library plus 3 µL water) and quantify by KAPA Library Quant [2][4]. (1 h)
  13. Pool libraries and sequence paired-end (2 × 50 bp or longer). Aim for more than 50 million mapped reads for open versus closed chromatin [2][3][4]. (variable)

Pause points

  • After the first cleanup, transposed DNA can be stored at −20 °C [2][4].
  • Final purified libraries can be stored at −20 °C [4].
  • Do not pause between lysis and transposition; transpose immediately [2].

Expected results

  • The Bioanalyzer trace shows nucleosomal banding: a nucleosome-free peak and a mono-nucleosome peak, with larger bands at lower intensity [2][3].
  • Libraries are typically about 10–40 nM by KAPA, or about 30 nM in 20 µL [2][5].
  • After sequencing, ENCODE expects TSS enrichment above 7 (human GRCh38), FRiP above 0.3, NRF and PBC1 above 0.9, and at least two biological replicates [3].

Controls

  • At least two biological replicates [3]
  • A 10× scaled-down or titrated cell number when optimizing a new cell type [2]
  • Optional: tagmented naked genomic DNA as a sequence-bias reference (general practice, not from the cited protocols)

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely causeSolution
Mostly large fragments, no bandingToo many cells (under-transposition)Reduce to about 25,000–75,000 cells [2]
Mostly short fragmentsToo few cells (over-transposition)Increase cell number or reduce Tn5 [2]
High mitochondrial readsStandard lysis or cell typeUse the Omni-ATAC detergents [1][2]
Low complexity, many duplicatesToo many PCR cyclesUse the qPCR-guided cycle number; more than 11 total cycles is a concern [2]
Primer dimers or fragments over 1,000 bpInsufficient cleanupUse double-sided SPRI cleanup: 0.5× then 1.3× [4]
High backgroundFree DNA from dead cellsDNase pretreatment at 200 U/mL, 37 °C, 30 min [1]
Inaccurate concentrationQubit with variable fragment sizesUse KAPA Library Quant [2]
Lost pelletVery small pelletUse clear low-bind tubes [5]

Safety and waste

  • Wear gloves and a lab coat; check safety data sheets for NP-40, digitonin (toxic) and SYBR Green I (in DMSO).
  • Dispose of ethanol, SYBR/DMSO and bead waste according to institutional chemical-waste rules.
  • Follow the biosafety rules for the cells or tissue you use.

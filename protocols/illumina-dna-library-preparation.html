Illumina DNA Library Preparation

· 3-4 hours · 2026-11-14

Bead-linked transposome tagmentation, post-tagmentation cleanup, indexed PCR and bead cleanup to build pooled, sequencing-ready Illumina libraries from genomic DNA.

Download Word (.docx)Download PowerPoint (.pptx)

Estimated time: 3–4 hours | Protocol World

To convert 1–500 ng of genomic DNA into dual-indexed, sequencing-ready Illumina libraries (insert size about 300–350 bp) using the Illumina DNA Prep workflow.

Transposomes bound to magnetic beads (BLT) simultaneously fragment DNA and add adapter sequences in one on-bead tagmentation reaction. Because the beads saturate at about 100 ng or more of input, library yield self-normalizes in that range [1]. After a wash, a reduced-cycle PCR adds unique dual indexes and full adapters, and a bead cleanup removes primers and size-selects the library before pooling [1][2][3][4].

A. Prepare

  • Remove reagents and thaw/equilibrate per the reference guide: BLT stays at 2–8 °C (never below 2 °C); TB1, EPM and RSB are kept at −25 to −15 °C; IPB, TSB, TWB at room temperature (warm TSB to 37 °C if precipitated) [2].
  • Quantify input DNA fluorometrically and dilute to 1–500 ng in up to 30 µL (10 mM Tris or water) [2]. Seal plates for all shaking, cycling and spinning steps [2].

B. Tagmentation

  • Vortex BLT well before use (do not spin it down before pipetting). Make a master mix of BLT and TB1, 11 µL each per sample (22 µL) [2].
  • Add 20 µL master mix to each 30 µL DNA sample and mix by pipetting 10 times [2].
  • Run the TAG program: 55 °C for 15 min, hold at 10 °C (50 µL reaction, heated lid 100 °C) [2].

C. Post-tagmentation cleanup

  • Add 10 µL TSB, mix 10 times and incubate at 37 °C for 15 min (PTC program, hold at 10 °C) [2].
  • Place on the magnet until clear (about 3 min) and discard the supernatant [2].
  • Wash the beads twice with 100 µL TWB, adding slowly to avoid foaming and removing the final wash fully [2].

D. Indexed PCR amplification

  • Add the PCR master mix (EPM plus water) and the unique index adapters (i7 and i5) to the washed beads and mix [2][4].
  • Amplify with a reduced-cycle program. The number of cycles depends on DNA input; consult the cycle table in the current reference guide (this guide's PCR table was not verified here). For comparison, an adapted rapid protocol uses 8 cycles [4], and a scaled-down metagenomic protocol uses 14 cycles of a different PCR mix for about 10 ng input [3].

E. Library cleanup, pooling and QC

  • Perform a double-sided bead cleanup (for example a first bead fraction to remove large fragments, then a second to capture the library) with IPB/SPB, wash twice with 80% ethanol and elute in RSB [2][3][4].
  • For 100–500 ng inputs, libraries can be pooled by equal volume without individual quantification; below 100 ng, quantify each library and pool equimolar amounts [1][2][3].
  • Check the pool on a Bioanalyzer or TapeStation: expect a smear centered near 400–700 bp with no adapter dimer peak [2][3].
  • Quantify the pool by Qubit, calculate molarity and dilute to the loading concentration of your sequencer per Illumina's instructions [2]. Store libraries at −25 to −15 °C.
  • Libraries with a peak insert of about 300–350 bp and a broad distribution roughly 250–1000 bp [1][2].
  • Pooled libraries free of adapter dimer, with concentrations sufficient for dilution to the sequencer's loading concentration.

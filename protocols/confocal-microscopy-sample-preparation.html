Confocal Microscopy Sample Preparation

Estimated time: 1.5 days | Protocol World

To prepare fixed, immunolabeled cultured cells on coverslips so they give clean, high-resolution optical sections on a confocal microscope.

Confocal imaging rejects out-of-focus light with a pinhole, so image quality depends on the sample itself: cells must sit on glass of the thickness the objective is corrected for (#1.5, 0.17 mm), keep their structure through fixation, and be labeled specifically with fluorophores that match the laser lines and do not overlap spectrally [1][2][3]. Aldehyde fixation preserves morphology, detergent or saponin opens membranes for antibodies, blocking reduces non-specific binding, and a mounting medium with antifade and a suitable refractive index protects the signal and matches the optics [1][2][3][4].

ItemDetails
Coverslips#1.5 (0.17 mm) round, 12–18 mm; sterilize in 70% ethanol or UV for 30 min; acid-washed coverslips are also used [1][2][4]
Coating (optional)Poly-lysine at 50 µg/mL for 1 h, or collagen/fibronectin, for weakly adherent cells; many lines grow on plain glass [1]
PBS1× phosphate-buffered saline, pH 7.4
Fixative4% formaldehyde (from methanol-free paraformaldehyde) in PBS; use fresh or freshly thawed. Alternative: ice-cold or −20 °C methanol for a few minutes (note: it can destroy GFP and some epitopes) [1][2][3]
Quenching solution15 mM glycine in PBS (or 10 mM NH₄Cl) to neutralize free aldehydes [1][3]
Permeabilization buffer0.1% saponin in PBS for 10 min, or 0.1–0.5% Triton X-100 in PBS for 5–10 min, depending on the antigen [1][3]
Blocking buffer1% BSA in PBS (plus 0.01% saponin if saponin is used) or 5% normal serum from the secondary antibody host species; do not use serum from the primary host [2][3]
Primary antibodyDiluted in blocking buffer, typically 1:100–1:500, about 20 µL per 12–13 mm coverslip [1][3]
Secondary antibodyFluorophore-conjugated, cross-adsorbed, about 1:200–1:500 in blocking buffer [1][3]
Nuclear counterstainDAPI at about 1 µg/mL in PBS for 5 min (check that the blue channel is free)
Mounting mediumAntifade mountant (for example, ProLong Gold or Fluoromount-G), or 50–90% glycerol in PBS, pH 8–9, with an antifade additive [1][2]
SealantClear nail polish or commercial sealant, for non-hardening mountants
  • Biosafety cabinet
  • CO₂ incubator
  • 12- or 24-well plates
  • Fine forceps and needle
  • Parafilm, humid dark box
  • Glass slides
  • Confocal microscope
  • Matched immersion oil
  • Sterilize #1.5 coverslips (70% ethanol or 30 min UV) and place one per well. Coat if needed, then rinse with PBS [1][4]. (45 min)
  • Seed cells onto the coverslips so they reach about 40–70% confluence at fixation; grow at least 6 h, preferably overnight [1][3]. (Overnight)
  • Rinse twice with warm PBS. Fix in 4% formaldehyde in PBS for 10–15 min at room temperature [1][3]. (15 min)
  • Rinse in PBS, then quench free aldehydes with 15 mM glycine in PBS, 2 × 5 min [3]. (10 min)
  • Permeabilize with 0.1% saponin for 10 min (or 0.1–0.5% Triton X-100 for 5–10 min) [1][3]. (10 min)
  • Block for 15–30 min in blocking buffer at room temperature [2][3]. (30 min)
  • Place 20 µL of primary antibody on parafilm in a humid chamber; invert the coverslip (cell side down) onto it. Incubate 1 h at room temperature or overnight at 4 °C [1][3]. (1 h–overnight)
  • Return coverslips to a plate and wash in blocking buffer or PBS with 0.05% Tween-20, 4 × 5 min [1][3]. (20 min)
  • Incubate with secondary antibody (20 µL, cell side down) for 45–60 min at room temperature, protected from light [1][3]. (45–60 min)
  • Wash 4 × 5 min in PBS with 0.05% Tween-20 in the dark; add DAPI for the last wash if desired [1][3]. (25 min)
  • Dip briefly in distilled water to remove salt, blot the edge, and mount cell side down on about 5 µL of mountant on a clean slide, avoiding bubbles [1][3]. (10 min)
  • Let hardening mountants cure flat in the dark (overnight is typical); seal the edges if the mountant does not harden [1][3]. (Overnight)
  • Image with a #1.5-corrected objective and appropriate immersion medium. Set the pinhole near 1 Airy unit, use sequential channel scanning, and keep settings identical across samples.
  • Store slides at 4 °C in the dark; image soon for best signal [1].
  • After fixation and quenching: coverslips can be stored in PBS at 4 °C for a few days.
  • After the primary antibody: overnight at 4 °C is acceptable.
  • After mounting: cured slides can be stored in the dark at 4 °C.
  • Cells with well-preserved morphology and specific, localized staining against a dark background.
  • Cleanly separated channels with no bleed-through when imaged sequentially.
  • Sharp optical sections without spherical aberration or fading.
  • Secondary-only coverslip for non-specific secondary binding
  • Knockout, RNAi, or untreated cells (or blocking peptide) to confirm antibody specificity
  • Single-fluorophore samples to set up and check cross-talk
  • Unlabeled cells to measure autofluorescence
ProblemLikely causeSolution
Weak or no signalEpitope masked or destroyed by fixation, insufficient permeabilization, antibody too diluteTry methanol or a different fixative, increase permeabilization, or lower dilution [1][3]
High backgroundInsufficient blocking or washing, secondary too concentrated, free aldehydesQuench with glycine, block longer, wash more, and titrate the secondary [1][3]
Salt crystals or hazeResidual PBS before mountingRinse in distilled water before mounting [1][3]
Cells detach or look distortedHarsh handling, poor adhesion, strong fixationCoat coverslips, handle gently, and test shorter fixation [1]
Blurry images at depthWrong coverslip thickness or mismatched mediumUse #1.5 coverslips and a matching immersion oil and mountant [2][4]
Bleed-through between channelsOverlapping fluorophoresChoose separated dyes, scan sequentially, and check single-stain controls [2]
  • Formaldehyde is toxic and a suspected carcinogen; handle in a fume hood or ventilated area and collect as hazardous waste.
  • Methanol is flammable and toxic.
  • Some mounting media and sodium azide are hazardous; wear gloves.
  • Dispose of coverslips and slides in sharps containers and treat all cell materials according to biosafety rules.

All protocols

  • Acid-Fast Staining – Ziehl–Neelsen (hot) and Kinyoun (cold) acid-fast staining of bacterial smears with carbolfuchsin, 3% acid–alcohol and methylene blue, including reagent recipes, timings, controls and safety notes.
  • Alkaline Phosphatase Assay – Measure alkaline phosphatase activity with pNPP, from a diethanolamine cuvette assay for purified enzyme to an end-point 96-well assay for lysates, tissue and serum, with standard curve and calculations.
  • Antibiotic Susceptibility Testing by Disk Diffusion – Standardized Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion on Mueller-Hinton agar: prepare a 0.5 McFarland inoculum, swab a uniform lawn, apply antibiotic disks, incubate at 35 °C, measure inhibition zones and interpret them against CLSI or EUCAST breakpoints with E. coli ATCC 25922 quality control.
  • Aseptic Technique and Biosafety Cabinet Use – Keep mammalian cell cultures sterile and the worker safe: Class II biosafety cabinet start-up, layout and airflow rules, sterile pipetting, waste handling, shutdown, contamination troubleshooting and cabinet certification.
  • Automated Cell Counting – Count mammalian cells and measure viability with an image-based automated counter and 0.4% trypan blue: 1:1 sample mixing, chamber loading, working range, viability and cell-number calculations, plus a hemocytometer cross-check.
  • BCA Protein Assay – Measure total protein in lysates with the bicinchoninic acid (BCA) assay in a 96-well plate: prepare BSA standards (25–2,000 µg/mL), mix the working reagent, incubate, read at 562 nm and calculate concentrations from the standard curve.
  • Bacterial Growth Curves – Measure E. coli growth over time by OD600 in shaken flasks with a cuvette spectrophotometer or in a 96-well plate reader, then calculate the specific growth rate and doubling time from the exponential phase.
  • Bacterial Streak Plating for Isolation – Pour LB agar plates and quadrant-streak E. coli to obtain single, well-separated colonies, with antibiotic selection, incubation and troubleshooting.
  • Beta-Galactosidase Activity Assay – Measure lacZ β-galactosidase activity in chloroform/SDS-permeabilized E. coli with ONPG and calculate Miller units from A420, A550, reaction time, culture volume and OD600.
  • Blue-White Screening – Identify recombinant E. coli colonies by lacZα complementation on X-gal/IPTG plates: plate preparation, transformation, incubation, scoring, controls, and confirmation of white colonies.
  • Bradford Protein Assay – Estimate protein concentration in a 96-well plate or cuvette with Coomassie G-250 and a BSA standard curve, including interference limits and curve fitting.
  • Buffer Preparation and pH Measurement – Calculate, dissolve, titrate and sterilize common buffers (Tris-HCl, PBS, EDTA, HEPES) and calibrate a pH meter for accurate readings.
  • C. elegans CRISPR Genome Editing – Edit a C. elegans gene by injecting preassembled Cas9–guide RNA complexes and a short single-stranded repair template into the gonad, using dpy-10 co-conversion to enrich edited F1 progeny, then genotype and homozygose the edit.
  • C. elegans Crossing and Male Maintenance – Generate and maintain C. elegans males by heat shock, then use them in small-spot mating crosses and confirm cross progeny by their ~50% male fraction and markers.
  • C. elegans Genotyping by Single-Worm PCR – Lyse a single C. elegans in proteinase K buffer and genotype it by direct PCR, with full recipes, cycling conditions, allele-specific SNP primer design, controls and troubleshooting.
  • C. elegans Maintenance on NGM Plates – Pour and seed NGM plates with OP50, keep C. elegans stocks by chunking or picking, and rescue contaminated cultures with a hypochlorite egg prep.
  • C. elegans Microinjection – Introduce plasmid DNA into the C. elegans distal gonad by microinjection to generate extrachromosomal-array transgenic lines, with a rol-6 or fluorescent co-injection marker, F1 screening and line maintenance.
  • C. elegans RNAi by Feeding – Knock down a C. elegans gene by feeding worms HT115(DE3) bacteria expressing dsRNA from L4440: IPTG-carbenicillin NGM plates, overnight induction, L1/L4 worm staging, scoring, controls (L4440, unc-22) and tissue limitations.
  • C. elegans Strain Freezing and Thawing – Cryopreserve C. elegans strains as just-starved L1–L2 larvae in S buffer with 15% glycerol, cool slowly at about 1 °C/min to −80 °C, test thaw the next day and recover on OP50 plates.
  • C. elegans Synchronization by Bleaching – Alkaline hypochlorite dissolves gravid adult C. elegans and releases eggshell-protected embryos, which hatch in M9 without food into a synchronized, arrested L1 population. Plating these L1s onto NGM/OP50 gives a cohort of the same age for assays.
  • Cell Counting with a Hemocytometer – Manual cell counting and trypan blue viability with an improved Neubauer hemocytometer: chamber setup, dilution, counting rules, calculations, acceptable ranges and troubleshooting.
  • Cell Sorting by FACS – Sort a defined live mammalian cell population by FACS: prepare a filtered single-cell suspension in sort buffer with viability dye, set nozzle and drop delay, gate out doublets, sort into serum-coated tubes and check purity.
  • ChIP-seq Library Preparation – Build barcoded Illumina libraries from 1–10 ng of ChIP and input DNA: end prep, adaptor titration, USER ligation, bead cleanup, input-based PCR cycle choice, QC and ENCODE depth/quality guidelines.
  • Competitive ELISA – Quantify a small antigen in cell culture supernatant by indirect competitive ELISA: coat antigen, pre-incubate sample with limiting primary antibody, detect with HRP and TMB, and fit an inverse four-parameter curve.
  • DAPI and Hoechst Nuclear Staining – Counterstain nuclei with DAPI in fixed cells or tissue sections and with cell-permeant Hoechst 33342 in live cells, then image with a UV/DAPI filter set.
  • DNase Treatment of RNA Samples – Remove contaminating genomic DNA from purified total RNA with RNase-free DNase I or TURBO DNase, inactivate the enzyme, and verify the RNA is DNA-free before RT-qPCR or RNA-seq.
  • Dual-Luciferase Assay – Sequential firefly and Renilla luciferase readout from one lysate of co-transfected HEK293T cells, using Promega DLR reagents or an in-house alternative, with ratio normalization to correct well-to-well variation.
  • ELISA (Indirect) – Coat, block, probe with primary and HRP-secondary antibodies, develop with TMB and read at 450 nm to quantify antigen or antibody in mammalian samples.
  • ELISpot Assay – Count cytokine-secreting cells (IFN-gamma, human PBMC) on PVDF plates: capture antibody, stimulation, detection, BCIP/NBT development and SFU calculation.
  • Electroporation of E. coli – Prepare electrocompetent E. coli by washing mid-log cells in ice-cold 10% glycerol, then transform plasmid DNA by a 1.8 kV pulse and recover in SOC.
  • Endospore Staining – Differentially stain bacterial endospores green and vegetative cells pink-red with malachite green and safranin using the Schaeffer–Fulton method, with B. subtilis as the spore-forming positive control and E. coli as the non-spore-forming negative control.
  • Enzyme Kinetics Michaelis-Menten Assay – Measure initial reaction rates of E. coli β-galactosidase with ONPG in a 96-well plate reader, convert absorbance with a product standard curve, and fit Km and Vmax by nonlinear regression with linearized-plot cross-checks.
  • Flow Cytometry Compensation and Gating – Set PMT voltages, build a compensation matrix from single-stained controls, use FMO controls and apply a reproducible gating hierarchy for multicolor flow cytometry of mammalian cells.
  • Flow Cytometry Sample Preparation – Prepare viable single-cell suspensions from mammalian cells, block Fc receptors, stain with a viability dye and fluorochrome-conjugated antibodies, and fix for flow cytometric analysis. Includes the controls needed for compensation and gating.
  • Freezing Cells for Cryostorage – Freeze mammalian cells for long-term storage: harvest in log phase, suspend in DMSO freezing medium, cool at about −1 °C per minute and keep in liquid nitrogen vapor.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Bacteria – Purify high-molecular-weight genomic DNA from bacteria with lysozyme, SDS, proteinase K, CTAB and phenol–chloroform extraction, then RNase treatment and ethanol precipitation.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Mammalian Cells – Proteinase K lysis followed by phenol-chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation to recover high-molecular-weight genomic DNA from cultured mammalian cells.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Plant Tissue – CTAB-based genomic DNA extraction from fresh or frozen plant leaf, with PVP and β-mercaptoethanol options for polysaccharide- and polyphenol-rich species.
  • Gibson Assembly – Seamless, one-pot joining of overlapping DNA fragments into a plasmid using T5 exonuclease, a high-fidelity polymerase and Taq ligase at 50 °C, followed by transformation into E. coli.
  • Golden Gate Assembly – One-pot Type IIS assembly of multiple DNA fragments with BsaI-HFv2 and T4 ligase, including thermocycling programs by fragment number, E. coli transformation and screening.
  • Gram Staining – Differential Gram staining of bacterial smears: smear preparation and fixation, crystal violet, iodine, decolorizer and safranin steps, oil-immersion reading, QC strains and troubleshooting.
  • Illumina DNA Library Preparation – Bead-linked transposome tagmentation, post-tagmentation cleanup, indexed PCR and bead cleanup to build pooled, sequencing-ready Illumina libraries from genomic DNA.
  • Illumina RNA-seq Library Preparation – Poly(A) capture, fragmentation, dUTP strand marking, adapter ligation, PCR and QC for strand-specific Illumina RNA-seq libraries.
  • Immunofluorescence of Tissue Sections – Detect a protein in fixed mouse tissue sections: fixation, cryosectioning, antigen retrieval, blocking, primary and fluorescent secondary antibodies, DAPI and antifade mounting.
  • Intracellular Cytokine Staining – Cytokine production by individual PBMCs or splenocytes is detected by short stimulation with transport inhibitors, then surface staining, fixation, permeabilization and flow cytometry.
  • Lactate Dehydrogenase (LDH) Assay – Two complementary LDH methods: a kinetic NADH-based activity assay read at 340 nm, and a colorimetric LDH-release cytotoxicity assay read at 490 nm. Includes unit calculations, % cytotoxicity and controls.
  • Luciferase Reporter Assay – Dual-luciferase reporter assay in transfected mammalian cells: co-transfect a firefly reporter and a Renilla control, lyse with passive lysis buffer, read both luciferases sequentially in a luminometer and normalize to calculate fold change.
  • LysoTracker Staining of Lysosomes – Label acidic lysosomes in live cells with 50–75 nM LysoTracker probes, load 30 min to 2 h at 37 °C, and image promptly with appropriate controls.
  • Methanol Fixation for Immunofluorescence – Cold-methanol fixation and permeabilization of adherent cells for indirect immunofluorescence: timing, blocking, antibody incubations, DAPI staining, mounting, controls and when to choose PFA instead.
  • Minimum Inhibitory Concentration (MIC) by Broth Microdilution – Determine the lowest antibiotic concentration that stops visible growth of an aerobic bacterial isolate: CAMHB 96-well 2-fold dilution series, 0.5 McFarland inoculum to 5 × 10⁵ CFU/mL, 16–20 h reading, growth/sterility/QC controls and troubleshooting.
  • MitoTracker Staining of Mitochondria – Label mitochondria in live cultured mammalian cells with MitoTracker dyes: stock preparation, working concentrations, staining and imaging, optional fixation, FCCP depolarization control and troubleshooting.
  • Mycoplasma Detection – Test mammalian cultures for mycoplasma with universal 16S rRNA PCR and Hoechst DNA staining of Vero indicator cells, with positive, negative and internal controls and interpretation guidance.
  • Native PAGE – Non-denaturing Tris-glycine polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis separates folded proteins and complexes by charge, size and shape. Run cold without SDS, reductant or heat, then stain, blot or assay in the gel.
  • Paraformaldehyde Fixation – Make 4% paraformaldehyde from powder and use it to fix cultured cells for immunofluorescence, including quenching with NH₄Cl, Triton X-100 permeabilization, suspension-cell and methanol alternatives, and safety notes.
  • Passaging Adherent Cells – Subculture an adherent mammalian cell line with trypsin-EDTA: wash, detach, neutralize, count, reseed by density or split ratio, with T-75 volumes and troubleshooting.
  • Passaging Suspension Cells – Subculture non-adherent mammalian cells such as Jurkat in log phase: count with trypan blue, calculate the carry-over volume, seed at a defined density by dilution or centrifugation, and feed or split every 2–3 days.
  • Permeabilization and Blocking for Immunostaining – Fix, permeabilize and block cultured cells for immunofluorescence: choosing Triton X-100, Tween-20, saponin or cold methanol, serum and BSA blocking recipes, timings, controls and a troubleshooting table.
  • Phalloidin Staining of F-Actin – Fix cells with methanol-free formaldehyde, permeabilize with Triton X-100 and stain filamentous actin with a fluorescent phalloidin conjugate, with optional DAPI or Hoechst counterstain and antifade mounting.
  • Plasmid Midiprep and Maxiprep – Alkaline lysis and anion-exchange purification of 100 µg to about 1 mg of plasmid DNA from 25–500 mL of E. coli culture, with culture volumes, buffer volumes, centrifugation settings and troubleshooting for midi and maxi scale.
  • Plasmid Miniprep by Alkaline Lysis – Isolate plasmid DNA from 2 mL of E. coli overnight culture with homemade P1, P2 and P3 buffers, isopropanol precipitation and an ethanol wash, then check yield, purity and identity.
  • Poly(A) Selection for RNA-seq – Oligo(dT) magnetic beads capture polyadenylated mRNA from eukaryotic total RNA (mammalian cells as the example) so that rRNA is removed before RNA-seq library prep. This generic workflow uses two binding rounds for high purity; kit-specific values are labeled.
  • Ponceau S Staining – Stain proteins reversibly on nitrocellulose or PVDF with 0.1% Ponceau S in 5% acetic acid to check western blot transfer and loading, then destain before immunodetection.
  • Preparation of Chemically Competent Cells – Make frozen chemically competent E. coli (DH5α, TOP10, XL1-Blue) by the CaCl₂ or Inoue method and test with pUC19 (10⁶–10⁸ cfu/µg).
  • Preparation of Electrocompetent Cells – Prepare a 100 mL batch of electrocompetent E. coli by harvesting at OD600 0.4–0.6, washing four times in cold 10% glycerol and freezing 30–50 µL aliquots, with electroporation settings, recovery and an efficiency test with pUC19.
  • Preparation of LB Medium and Agar Plates – Mixing, autoclaving and pouring Lysogeny Broth (Lennox) and LB agar plates, with antibiotic addition at about 50 °C.
  • Preparation of NGM Plates and OP50 Lawns – Pour NGM agar, dry it, and seed a thin OP50 lawn for maintaining C. elegans, with recipes, volumes and troubleshooting.
  • Preparation of Stock Solutions and Dilutions – Calculate, make, label and store stock solutions, then prepare working and serial dilutions accurately.
  • Protein Extraction from Mammalian Cells (RIPA Lysis) – Ice-cold RIPA lysis with protease and phosphatase inhibitors, clarification by centrifugation and BCA quantification to prepare lysates for Western blotting and immunoassays.
  • Protein Extraction from Tissue – Homogenize frozen tissue in ice-cold RIPA buffer with protease and phosphatase inhibitors, clarify, quantify by BCA, and prepare samples for Western blotting.
  • RNA Extraction with Silica Spin Columns – Purify total RNA from cultured cells with guanidine lysis and a silica spin column, with optional on-column DNase, quality checks and troubleshooting.
  • RNA Integrity Assessment by Bioanalyzer or TapeStation – Assess RNA concentration and integrity on the Agilent Bioanalyzer (RNA 6000 Nano) or TapeStation (RNA and High Sensitivity RNA ScreenTape): sample and chip preparation, run settings, and interpretation of RIN, RINe and DV200 before RNA-seq.
  • Restriction-Ligation Subcloning – Move a DNA fragment from a donor plasmid or PCR product into a recipient vector using restriction enzymes and T4 DNA ligase, then transform E. coli and verify clones.
  • SDS-PAGE – Discontinuous Laemmli SDS-PAGE: gel recipes for 8–15% resolving and 5% stacking gels, sample preparation, running conditions, Coomassie staining, controls and troubleshooting.
  • Sandwich ELISA – Quantify a secreted cytokine such as IL-6 or TNF-α in cell-culture supernatant with a 96-well sandwich ELISA, TMB/HRP detection and a 4PL standard curve.
  • Semi-Dry Transfer of Proteins – Transfer SDS-PAGE proteins to PVDF or nitrocellulose in a plate-electrode semi-dry blotter: buffer recipes, membrane and stack assembly, voltage and time guidelines, Ponceau S verification and troubleshooting.
  • Serial Dilution and Viable Plate Counts – Count living bacteria by making tenfold serial dilutions, plating by spread or drop plate method, counting colonies in the countable range and calculating CFU/mL.
  • Single-Cell RNA-seq Sample Preparation – Prepare a viable, debris-free single-cell suspension from fresh tissue for droplet-based scRNA-seq: dissociation, filtration, RBC and dead-cell removal, counting and concentration adjustment to 1,000–1,500 cells/µL.
  • Single-Nucleus RNA-seq Nuclei Isolation – Isolate intact nuclei from flash-frozen tissue for single-nucleus RNA-seq: Dounce homogenization in cold sucrose-based buffer with RNase inhibitor, detergent lysis, BSA-cushion cleanup, filtration, and AO/PI counting to reach 700–1,200 nuclei/µL for droplet platforms such as 10x Chromium.
  • Small RNA-seq Library Preparation – Build Illumina-style small RNA (miRNA) sequencing libraries by gel size selection, 3′ and 5′ adapter ligation, reverse transcription, minimal-cycle PCR and gel purification, with QC and troubleshooting.
  • Spectrophotometric Enzyme Assays – Continuous cuvette assay following NADH at 340 nm, with linear-rate selection, controls and U/mL and specific-activity calculations (LDH example).
  • Strand-Specific RNA-seq – A kit-agnostic dUTP workflow for building strand-specific Illumina RNA-seq libraries from total RNA, with QC and strandedness checks. The second cDNA strand is marked with uracil and removed before PCR so that read orientation reflects the transcript strand.
  • Surface Marker Staining for Flow Cytometry – Stain cell-surface antigens on live mammalian cells for flow cytometry: antibody titration, Fc block, 30 min cold staining in the dark, washes, viability dye, optional paraformaldehyde fixation, with unstained, FMO and compensation controls.
  • TA Cloning – Clone a Taq-amplified PCR product into a pGEM-T style T-vector: insert:vector calculation, optional A-tailing, ligation, heat-shock transformation of lacZΔM15 E. coli, blue/white screening and colony PCR confirmation.
  • TOPO Cloning – Insert a Taq-amplified PCR product into a TOPO TA vector in 5 minutes without ligase, transform One Shot TOP10 E. coli, and screen blue/white colonies by colony PCR or EcoRI digestion.
  • Thawing Cryopreserved Cells – Rapid 37 °C thaw, aseptic dilution to remove DMSO, viability count and seeding of cryopreserved mammalian cells for reliable recovery.
  • Trypan Blue Viability Assay – A quick dye-exclusion method to count mammalian cells and estimate the percentage that are alive using 0.4% trypan blue and a hemocytometer. Includes harvesting, 1:1 staining, grid counting and a worked calculation.
  • Wet Tank Transfer of Proteins – Electroblot SDS-PAGE gels onto PVDF or nitrocellulose in a submerged tank, with buffer, methanol/SDS, voltage and cooling adjusted to protein size, and confirm transfer with Ponceau S.
  • Whole-Mount Immunostaining – Indirect fluorescent immunostaining of intact fixed embryos, larvae or small organs, with zebrafish as the worked example, permeabilization options, blocking, washes and glycerol clearing.
  • mRNA Purification with Oligo(dT) Beads – Poly(A)+ mRNA is captured from total RNA on oligo(dT) magnetic beads in high-salt buffer, washed and eluted in low-salt Tris at 80 °C. A second round of binding removes residual rRNA.
  • rRNA Depletion for RNA-seq – Probe hybridization, RNase H and DNase I digestion and bead cleanup to remove ribosomal RNA from total RNA before RNA-seq library preparation (NEBNext workflow).

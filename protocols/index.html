All protocols

  • BCA Protein Assay – Measure total protein in lysates with the bicinchoninic acid (BCA) assay in a 96-well plate: prepare BSA standards (25–2,000 µg/mL), mix the working reagent, incubate, read at 562 nm and calculate concentrations from the standard curve.
  • Bacterial Growth Curves – Measure E. coli growth over time by OD600 in shaken flasks with a cuvette spectrophotometer or in a 96-well plate reader, then calculate the specific growth rate and doubling time from the exponential phase.
  • Bacterial Streak Plating for Isolation – Pour LB agar plates and quadrant-streak E. coli to obtain single, well-separated colonies, with antibiotic selection, incubation and troubleshooting.
  • Bradford Protein Assay – Estimate protein concentration in a 96-well plate or cuvette with Coomassie G-250 and a BSA standard curve, including interference limits and curve fitting.
  • Buffer Preparation and pH Measurement – Calculate, dissolve, titrate and sterilize common buffers (Tris-HCl, PBS, EDTA, HEPES) and calibrate a pH meter for accurate readings.
  • C. elegans Maintenance on NGM Plates – Pour and seed NGM plates with OP50, keep C. elegans stocks by chunking or picking, and rescue contaminated cultures with a hypochlorite egg prep.
  • C. elegans Synchronization by Bleaching – Alkaline hypochlorite dissolves gravid adult C. elegans and releases eggshell-protected embryos, which hatch in M9 without food into a synchronized, arrested L1 population. Plating these L1s onto NGM/OP50 gives a cohort of the same age for assays.
  • Competitive ELISA – Quantify a small antigen in cell culture supernatant by indirect competitive ELISA: coat antigen, pre-incubate sample with limiting primary antibody, detect with HRP and TMB, and fit an inverse four-parameter curve.
  • ELISA (Indirect) – Coat, block, probe with primary and HRP-secondary antibodies, develop with TMB and read at 450 nm to quantify antigen or antibody in mammalian samples.
  • Electroporation of E. coli – Prepare electrocompetent E. coli by washing mid-log cells in ice-cold 10% glycerol, then transform plasmid DNA by a 1.8 kV pulse and recover in SOC.
  • Enzyme Kinetics Michaelis-Menten Assay – Measure initial reaction rates of E. coli β-galactosidase with ONPG in a 96-well plate reader, convert absorbance with a product standard curve, and fit Km and Vmax by nonlinear regression with linearized-plot cross-checks.
  • Freezing Cells for Cryostorage – Freeze mammalian cells for long-term storage: harvest in log phase, suspend in DMSO freezing medium, cool at about −1 °C per minute and keep in liquid nitrogen vapor.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Bacteria – Purify high-molecular-weight genomic DNA from bacteria with lysozyme, SDS, proteinase K, CTAB and phenol–chloroform extraction, then RNase treatment and ethanol precipitation.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Mammalian Cells – Proteinase K lysis followed by phenol-chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation to recover high-molecular-weight genomic DNA from cultured mammalian cells.
  • Genomic DNA Extraction from Plant Tissue – CTAB-based genomic DNA extraction from fresh or frozen plant leaf, with PVP and β-mercaptoethanol options for polysaccharide- and polyphenol-rich species.
  • Illumina DNA Library Preparation – Bead-linked transposome tagmentation, post-tagmentation cleanup, indexed PCR and bead cleanup to build pooled, sequencing-ready Illumina libraries from genomic DNA.
  • Illumina RNA-seq Library Preparation – Poly(A) capture, fragmentation, dUTP strand marking, adapter ligation, PCR and QC for strand-specific Illumina RNA-seq libraries.
  • Immunofluorescence of Tissue Sections – Detect a protein in fixed mouse tissue sections: fixation, cryosectioning, antigen retrieval, blocking, primary and fluorescent secondary antibodies, DAPI and antifade mounting.
  • Paraformaldehyde Fixation – Make 4% paraformaldehyde from powder and use it to fix cultured cells for immunofluorescence, including quenching with NH₄Cl, Triton X-100 permeabilization, suspension-cell and methanol alternatives, and safety notes.
  • Passaging Adherent Cells – Subculture an adherent mammalian cell line with trypsin-EDTA: wash, detach, neutralize, count, reseed by density or split ratio, with T-75 volumes and troubleshooting.
  • Plasmid Miniprep by Alkaline Lysis – Isolate plasmid DNA from 2 mL of E. coli overnight culture with homemade P1, P2 and P3 buffers, isopropanol precipitation and an ethanol wash, then check yield, purity and identity.
  • Poly(A) Selection for RNA-seq – Oligo(dT) magnetic beads capture polyadenylated mRNA from eukaryotic total RNA (mammalian cells as the example) so that rRNA is removed before RNA-seq library prep. This generic workflow uses two binding rounds for high purity; kit-specific values are labeled.
  • Preparation of Chemically Competent Cells – Make frozen chemically competent E. coli (DH5α, TOP10, XL1-Blue) by the CaCl₂ or Inoue method and test with pUC19 (10⁶–10⁸ cfu/µg).
  • Preparation of Electrocompetent Cells – Prepare a 100 mL batch of electrocompetent E. coli by harvesting at OD600 0.4–0.6, washing four times in cold 10% glycerol and freezing 30–50 µL aliquots, with electroporation settings, recovery and an efficiency test with pUC19.
  • Preparation of LB Medium and Agar Plates – Mixing, autoclaving and pouring Lysogeny Broth (Lennox) and LB agar plates, with antibiotic addition at about 50 °C.
  • Preparation of NGM Plates and OP50 Lawns – Pour NGM agar, dry it, and seed a thin OP50 lawn for maintaining C. elegans, with recipes, volumes and troubleshooting.
  • Preparation of Stock Solutions and Dilutions – Calculate, make, label and store stock solutions, then prepare working and serial dilutions accurately.
  • Protein Extraction from Mammalian Cells (RIPA Lysis) – Ice-cold RIPA lysis with protease and phosphatase inhibitors, clarification by centrifugation and BCA quantification to prepare lysates for Western blotting and immunoassays.
  • Protein Extraction from Tissue – Homogenize frozen tissue in ice-cold RIPA buffer with protease and phosphatase inhibitors, clarify, quantify by BCA, and prepare samples for Western blotting.
  • Sandwich ELISA – Quantify a secreted cytokine such as IL-6 or TNF-α in cell-culture supernatant with a 96-well sandwich ELISA, TMB/HRP detection and a 4PL standard curve.
  • Thawing Cryopreserved Cells – Rapid 37 °C thaw, aseptic dilution to remove DMSO, viability count and seeding of cryopreserved mammalian cells for reliable recovery.
  • Whole-Mount Immunostaining – Indirect fluorescent immunostaining of intact fixed embryos, larvae or small organs, with zebrafish as the worked example, permeabilization options, blocking, washes and glycerol clearing.

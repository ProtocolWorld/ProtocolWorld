All protocols

  • ATAC-seq – Omni-ATAC-seq library preparation from 50,000 cells: nuclei lysis with digitonin/Tween-20, Tn5 tagmentation, qPCR-guided amplification, cleanup, QC and sequencing guidance for mapping open chromatin.
  • Acid-Fast Staining – Ziehl–Neelsen (hot) and Kinyoun (cold) acid-fast staining of bacterial smears with carbolfuchsin, 3% acid–alcohol and methylene blue, including reagent recipes, timings, controls and safety notes.
  • Alkaline Phosphatase Assay – Measure alkaline phosphatase activity with pNPP, from a diethanolamine cuvette assay for purified enzyme to an end-point 96-well assay for lysates, tissue and serum, with standard curve and calculations.
  • Antibiotic Susceptibility Testing by Disk Diffusion – Standardized Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion on Mueller-Hinton agar: prepare a 0.5 McFarland inoculum, swab a uniform lawn, apply antibiotic disks, incubate at 35 °C, measure inhibition zones and interpret them against CLSI or EUCAST breakpoints with E. coli ATCC 25922 quality control.
  • Aseptic Technique and Biosafety Cabinet Use – Keep mammalian cell cultures sterile and the worker safe: Class II biosafety cabinet start-up, layout and airflow rules, sterile pipetting, waste handling, shutdown, contamination troubleshooting and cabinet certification.
  • Automated Cell Counting – Count mammalian cells and measure viability with an image-based automated counter and 0.4% trypan blue: 1:1 sample mixing, chamber loading, working range, viability and cell-number calculations, plus a hemocytometer cross-check.
  • BCA Protein Assay – Measure total protein in lysates with the bicinchoninic acid (BCA) assay in a 96-well plate: prepare BSA standards (25–2,000 µg/mL), mix the working reagent, incubate, read at 562 nm and calculate concentrations from the standard curve.
  • Bacterial Conjugation – Transfer a mobilizable plasmid from an E. coli donor (WM3064/MFDpir DAP auxotroph, or a triparental helper format) into a recipient by plate mating, then select, counter-select and confirm exconjugants by PCR.
  • Bacterial Glycerol Stocks – Prepare long-term −80 °C glycerol stocks of E. coli strains and plasmid-carrying clones, label and record them, and revive them correctly by scraping without thawing.
  • Bacterial Growth Curves – Measure E. coli growth over time by OD600 in shaken flasks with a cuvette spectrophotometer or in a 96-well plate reader, then calculate the specific growth rate and doubling time from the exponential phase.
  • Bacterial Motility Assays – Measure swimming, swarming and twitching motility on semi-solid agar. Agar concentrations, plate drying, stab and spot inoculation, incubation, ImageJ measurement and controls for Pseudomonas, E. coli, Bacillus and Salmonella.
  • Bacterial Streak Plating for Isolation – Pour LB agar plates and quadrant-streak E. coli to obtain single, well-separated colonies, with antibiotic selection, incubation and troubleshooting.
  • Bacteriophage Lysate Preparation – Prepare a high-titer, cell-free bacteriophage lysate from E. coli by plate (web-lysis) or liquid-culture amplification: buffers, plating density, clarification, filtration, storage, titer check and troubleshooting.
  • Bacteriophage Plaque Assay – Titer bacteriophage by the double agar overlay: dilute phage in SM buffer, mix with log-phase E. coli in soft agar, pour on bottom agar, count plaques and calculate PFU/mL.
  • Beta-Galactosidase Activity Assay – Measure lacZ β-galactosidase activity in chloroform/SDS-permeabilized E. coli with ONPG and calculate Miller units from A420, A550, reaction time, culture volume and OD600.
  • Biofilm Crystal Violet Assay – Static 96-well biofilm assay: grow E. coli or other bacteria 24 h, wash away planktonic cells, stain with 0.1% crystal violet, dissolve in 30% acetic acid and read A550 to quantify biofilm biomass.
  • Blocking and Antibody Incubation for Western Blots – Block a western blot membrane with 5% milk or BSA in TBST, incubate primary and HRP or fluorescent secondary antibodies, wash, and detect, with troubleshooting for background and weak signal.
  • Blue-White Screening – Identify recombinant E. coli colonies by lacZα complementation on X-gal/IPTG plates: plate preparation, transformation, incubation, scoring, controls, and confirmation of white colonies.
  • Bone Marrow Cell Isolation – Isolate single-cell bone marrow suspensions from adult mouse femurs and tibias by syringe flushing, with ACK red-cell lysis, filtering and counting, plus a rapid centrifugation alternative.
  • Bone Marrow-Derived Macrophage Differentiation – Differentiate mouse bone marrow progenitors into macrophages over 7 days using L929-conditioned medium: marrow harvest, ACK lysis, seeding on non-TC plastic, feeding, EDTA detachment, replating, purity check and troubleshooting.
  • Bradford Protein Assay – Estimate protein concentration in a 96-well plate or cuvette with Coomassie G-250 and a BSA standard curve, including interference limits and curve fitting.
  • Buffer Preparation and pH Measurement – Calculate, dissolve, titrate and sterilize common buffers (Tris-HCl, PBS, EDTA, HEPES) and calibrate a pH meter for accurate readings.
  • C. elegans Brood Size Assay – Follow single C. elegans hermaphrodites from L4, transfer every 24 h for 5 days, count living progeny, dead eggs and unfertilized eggs, and compare brood size and embryonic lethality with wild type.
  • C. elegans CRISPR Genome Editing – Edit a C. elegans gene by injecting preassembled Cas9–guide RNA complexes and a short single-stranded repair template into the gonad, using dpy-10 co-conversion to enrich edited F1 progeny, then genotype and homozygose the edit.
  • C. elegans Crossing and Male Maintenance – Generate and maintain C. elegans males by heat shock, then use them in small-spot mating crosses and confirm cross progeny by their ~50% male fraction and markers.
  • C. elegans Embryo Imaging – Mount and time-lapse image living C. elegans embryos on agar pads or 20 µm bead spacers, with low-phototoxicity settings, stage timing and troubleshooting.
  • C. elegans Genotyping by Single-Worm PCR – Lyse a single C. elegans in proteinase K buffer and genotype it by direct PCR, with full recipes, cycling conditions, allele-specific SNP primer design, controls and troubleshooting.
  • C. elegans Germline Dissection – Extrude gonads from young adult hermaphrodites with two needles in levamisole/PBS, fix with 3% formaldehyde then −20 °C methanol, stain with antibodies and DAPI, and mount on agar pads to image the germline.
  • C. elegans Gonad Immunostaining – Dissect adult C. elegans gonads, fix with PFA, freeze-crack, methanol permeabilize, and stain with primary and fluorescent secondary antibodies and DAPI to image germline protein localization and chromosomes.
  • C. elegans Lifespan Assay – Measure survival of a synchronized C. elegans cohort at 20 °C on FUdR plates: NGM and OP50 preparation, egg-lay or bleach synchronization, L4 transfer, scoring and censoring rules, Kaplan–Meier/log-rank analysis, controls and troubleshooting.
  • C. elegans Maintenance on NGM Plates – Pour and seed NGM plates with OP50, keep C. elegans stocks by chunking or picking, and rescue contaminated cultures with a hypochlorite egg prep.
  • C. elegans Microinjection – Introduce plasmid DNA into the C. elegans distal gonad by microinjection to generate extrachromosomal-array transgenic lines, with a rol-6 or fluorescent co-injection marker, F1 screening and line maintenance.
  • C. elegans RNAi by Feeding – Knock down a C. elegans gene by feeding worms HT115(DE3) bacteria expressing dsRNA from L4440: IPTG-carbenicillin NGM plates, overnight induction, L1/L4 worm staging, scoring, controls (L4440, unc-22) and tissue limitations.
  • C. elegans RNAi by Soaking – Synthesize dsRNA from a T7/T3 template, soak L4 or L1 C. elegans for 24 h in spermidine-gelatin buffer, recover on OP50 and score P0 and F1 phenotypes with proper controls.
  • C. elegans Strain Freezing and Thawing – Cryopreserve C. elegans strains as just-starved L1–L2 larvae in S buffer with 15% glycerol, cool slowly at about 1 °C/min to −80 °C, test thaw the next day and recover on OP50 plates.
  • C. elegans Synchronization by Bleaching – Alkaline hypochlorite dissolves gravid adult C. elegans and releases eggshell-protected embryos, which hatch in M9 without food into a synchronized, arrested L1 population. Plating these L1s onto NGM/OP50 gives a cohort of the same age for assays.
  • CUT&RUN – Profile histone marks or transcription factors from up to 500,000 mammalian cells: bind digitonin-permeabilized cells to ConA beads, target pAG-MNase with an antibody, cut with Ca²⁺ at 0 °C, purify released fragments with spike-in and controls.
  • CUT&Tag – Bind up to 500,000 cells to ConA beads, permeabilize with digitonin, tether pA-Tn5 with primary and secondary antibodies, tagment at 37 °C with Mg²⁺, extract DNA, amplify 13 PCR cycles and check the library for sequencing.
  • Calcium Phosphate Transfection – Transfect HEK293/293T cells with plasmid DNA using a calcium phosphate–DNA coprecipitate: buffer recipes and pH 7.05, 10-cm and 6-well volumes, timing, chloroquine option, controls and troubleshooting.
  • Cell Counting with a Hemocytometer – Manual cell counting and trypan blue viability with an improved Neubauer hemocytometer: chamber setup, dilution, counting rules, calculations, acceptable ranges and troubleshooting.
  • Cell Sorting by FACS – Sort a defined live mammalian cell population by FACS: prepare a filtered single-cell suspension in sort buffer with viability dye, set nozzle and drop delay, gate out doublets, sort into serum-coated tubes and check purity.
  • ChIP-seq Library Preparation – Build barcoded Illumina libraries from 1–10 ng of ChIP and input DNA: end prep, adaptor titration, USER ligation, bead cleanup, input-based PCR cycle choice, QC and ENCODE depth/quality guidelines.
  • Coating Plates with Collagen or Fibronectin – Coat plastic or glass culture surfaces with rat tail collagen I or fibronectin to improve attachment and spreading of adherent mammalian cells. Covers dilution, vessel volumes, incubation, rinsing, sterility, storage and seeding.
  • Colony PCR – Screen bacterial colonies for the presence and size of a cloned insert by PCR directly from a picked colony, with a 20 µL reaction, cycling guidelines, a patch plate to keep the clone, and gel analysis.
  • Competitive ELISA – Quantify a small antigen in cell culture supernatant by indirect competitive ELISA: coat antigen, pre-incubate sample with limiting primary antibody, detect with HRP and TMB, and fit an inverse four-parameter curve.
  • Confocal Microscopy Sample Preparation – Prepare fixed, immunolabeled cultured cells on #1.5 coverslips for confocal imaging: fixation, quenching, permeabilization, blocking, antibody staining, DAPI, antifade mounting, and imaging tips.
  • Coomassie Blue Staining – Visualize proteins separated by SDS-PAGE with classic Coomassie R-250 or colloidal G-250 stain. Covers fixing, staining, destaining, imaging, storage and mass spectrometry notes.
  • DAPI and Hoechst Nuclear Staining – Counterstain nuclei with DAPI in fixed cells or tissue sections and with cell-permeant Hoechst 33342 in live cells, then image with a UV/DAPI filter set.
  • DNase Treatment of RNA Samples – Remove contaminating genomic DNA from purified total RNA with RNase-free DNase I or TURBO DNase, inactivate the enzyme, and verify the RNA is DNA-free before RT-qPCR or RNA-seq.
  • Dendritic Cell Generation from Bone Marrow – Differentiate mouse bone marrow into dendritic cells with GM-CSF: bone flush, RBC lysis, culture in bacteriological dishes with partial feeding, harvest around day 8, and CD11c/CD11b flow check.
  • Dual-Luciferase Assay – Sequential firefly and Renilla luciferase readout from one lysate of co-transfected HEK293T cells, using Promega DLR reagents or an in-house alternative, with ratio normalization to correct well-to-well variation.
  • ECL Chemiluminescent Detection – Detect HRP-labeled antibodies on a western blot with enhanced chemiluminescence (ECL): final washes, substrate preparation at 0.1 mL/cm², and film or digital imaging with exposure series and troubleshooting.
  • ELISA (Indirect) – Coat, block, probe with primary and HRP-secondary antibodies, develop with TMB and read at 450 nm to quantify antigen or antibody in mammalian samples.
  • ELISpot Assay – Count cytokine-secreting cells (IFN-gamma, human PBMC) on PVDF plates: capture antibody, stimulation, detection, BCIP/NBT development and SFU calculation.
  • Electrophoretic Mobility Shift Assay (EMSA) – Detect protein–DNA binding with a 3′-biotin probe: 5% native gel, binding buffer with poly(dI·dC), 15 + 60 min at 25 °C, 50–300× cold competitor, transfer, UV crosslink and chemiluminescent detection.
  • Electroporation of E. coli – Prepare electrocompetent E. coli by washing mid-log cells in ice-cold 10% glycerol, then transform plasmid DNA by a 1.8 kV pulse and recover in SOC.
  • Electroporation of Mammalian Cells – Electroporate mammalian cells in 0.4 cm cuvettes: log-phase cells, ice-cold buffer, 10–40 µg DNA, cell-type starting pulse settings, recovery, and viability and efficiency checks.
  • Endospore Staining – Differentially stain bacterial endospores green and vegetative cells pink-red with malachite green and safranin using the Schaeffer–Fulton method, with B. subtilis as the spore-forming positive control and E. coli as the non-spore-forming negative control.
  • Enzyme Kinetics Michaelis-Menten Assay – Measure initial reaction rates of E. coli β-galactosidase with ONPG in a 96-well plate reader, convert absorbance with a product standard curve, and fit Km and Vmax by nonlinear regression with linearized-plot cross-checks.
  • FRAP (Fluorescence Recovery After Photobleaching) – Measure protein mobility in live mammalian cells: bleach an EGFP-fusion ROI on a confocal microscope, image recovery, correct and normalize, and fit mobile fraction and half-time.
  • FRET Imaging – Live-cell sensitized-emission FRET imaging of a CFP/YFP-type pair in HEK293 cells: transfection, single-label and tandem controls, fixed acquisition settings, cross-talk correction, normalized FRET indices and troubleshooting.
  • Flow Cytometry Compensation and Gating – Set PMT voltages, build a compensation matrix from single-stained controls, use FMO controls and apply a reproducible gating hierarchy for multicolor flow cytometry of mammalian cells.

  • Fluorescence Polarization Assay – Calibrate the G factor, pick a tracer concentration, run a direct-binding titration for Kd and a competition titration for IC50 in black 384-well plates, and check mP window and Z′.
  • Fluorescent Western Blotting – Detect and quantify one or two proteins per blot with near-infrared fluorescent secondaries: low-fluorescence membrane, detergent-free blocking, 1 h dark secondary incubation, two-channel scanning, and normalization to total protein.
  • Freezing Cells for Cryostorage – Freeze mammalian cells for long-term storage: harvest in log phase, suspend in DMSO freezing medium, cool at about −1 °C per minute and keep in liquid nitrogen vapor.

  • Touchdown PCR – Run touchdown PCR to boost specificity: Tm calculation, hot-start reaction setup, 12 cycles stepping the annealing temperature down 1 °C each cycle, a plateau phase, gel check, optimization for GC-rich and degenerate primers.
  • Trypan Blue Viability Assay – A quick dye-exclusion method to count mammalian cells and estimate the percentage that are alive using 0.4% trypan blue and a hemocytometer. Includes harvesting, 1:1 staining, grid counting and a worked calculation.
  • Verification of Clones by Restriction Digest – Confirm cloning success with an analytical restriction digest: predict fragment sizes, set up 20 µL digests with an uncut control, run a 1% agarose gel, interpret supercoiled versus digested lanes, check orientation, and follow with sequencing.
  • Wet Tank Transfer of Proteins – Electroblot SDS-PAGE gels onto PVDF or nitrocellulose in a submerged tank, with buffer, methanol/SDS, voltage and cooling adjusted to protein size, and confirm transfer with Ponceau S.
  • Whole-Genome Bisulfite Sequencing – Prepare a methylated-adapter WGBS library for single-base methylation mapping: gDNA with lambda spike-in, shearing, end repair, A-tailing, ligation, bisulfite conversion, uracil-tolerant PCR, QC, sequencing depth and conversion-efficiency checks.
  • Whole-Mount Immunostaining – Indirect fluorescent immunostaining of intact fixed embryos, larvae or small organs, with zebrafish as the worked example, permeabilization options, blocking, washes and glycerol clearing.
  • cDNA Synthesis by Reverse Transcription – A two-step, 20 µL first-strand cDNA synthesis from purified total RNA using oligo(dT), random hexamer or gene-specific priming, a thermostable reverse transcriptase and an RNase inhibitor. The cDNA is ready for qPCR or PCR, with no-RT and no-template controls.
  • mRNA Purification with Oligo(dT) Beads – Poly(A)+ mRNA is captured from total RNA on oligo(dT) magnetic beads in high-salt buffer, washed and eluted in low-salt Tris at 80 °C. A second round of binding removes residual rRNA.
  • rRNA Depletion for RNA-seq – Probe hybridization, RNase H and DNase I digestion and bead cleanup to remove ribosomal RNA from total RNA before RNA-seq library preparation (NEBNext workflow).

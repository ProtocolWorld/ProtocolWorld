mRNA Purification with Oligo(dT) Beads

General Lab Methods · About 2.5 h (1.5 h without QC and second round) · 2026-11-21

Poly(A)+ mRNA is captured from total RNA on oligo(dT) magnetic beads in high-salt buffer, washed and eluted in low-salt Tris at 80 °C. A second round of binding removes residual rRNA.

mRNA Purification with Oligo(dT) Beads

Estimated time: About 2.5 h (1.5 h without QC and second round) | Protocol World

To enrich polyadenylated mRNA from total RNA (or crude lysate) to separate it from rRNA and tRNA for RNA-seq library preparation, RT-qPCR, cDNA synthesis or cloning.

Poly(A) tails of mRNA base-pair with oligo(dT) covalently attached to paramagnetic beads in high-salt buffer, while rRNA and tRNA stay in solution. Briefly heating RNA to 65 °C and chilling it opens secondary structure so the tails are accessible [2][3][5]. High-salt washes remove unbound RNA, and low-salt Tris or water at 75–80 °C breaks the A–T pairing and releases the mRNA [2][3][4]. A second bind–elute round markedly lowers rRNA carryover [2][3]. The beads can also be kept loaded for on-bead cDNA synthesis [4][5].

ItemDetails
Total RNA1–5 µg, DNA-free, RIN ≥ 7 (up to 10 µg with larger bead volumes)
Oligo(dT)25 magnetic beads20 µL per 1–5 µg RNA (Dynabeads, NEB or equivalent); do not freeze or dry beads
Binding buffer20 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 1.0 M LiCl, 2 mM EDTA; about 330 µL per sample
Wash buffer10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 0.15 M LiCl, 1 mM EDTA; about 700 µL per sample
Elution buffer10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 or nuclease-free water; 40–70 µL per sample
Lysis/binding buffer (crude lysate only)100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 500 mM LiCl, 10 mM EDTA, 1% LiDS, 5 mM DTT
Nuclease-free waterFor RNA dilution
RNase-removal reagentTo decontaminate surfaces and pipettes
RNase-free filter tips and 0.2 mL tubesLow-binding, nuclease-free
RNase inhibitor (optional)May be added at any step
  • Magnetic rack for 0.2 or 1.5 mL tubes
  • Thermal cycler or two heat blocks (65 °C and 80 °C)
  • Tube rotator or thermomixer
  • Micropipettes (2–200 µL)
  • NanoDrop or fluorometer
  • Agilent Bioanalyzer with RNA Pico chip
  • Ice bucket
  • −80 °C freezer
  • Decontaminate bench, pipettes and magnetic rack with an RNase-removal reagent; wear gloves and use RNase-free filter tips and tubes [1][3][4]. (10 min)
  • Prepare binding buffer (20 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 1.0 M LiCl, 2 mM EDTA) and wash buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 0.15 M LiCl, 1 mM EDTA) in RNase-free water [3][4]. (15 min)
  • Preheat one heat block or thermal cycler to 65 °C and another to 80 °C; chill a rack of ice [2][3]. (5 min)
  • Dilute 1–5 µg DNA-free total RNA to 50 µL with nuclease-free water in a 0.2 mL tube; keep at 4 °C until use [2][3]. (5 min)
  • Denature the RNA at 65 °C for 5 min to open secondary structure, then snap-cool on ice [2][3]. Use 2 min at 65 °C for crude, high-input samples [4]. (8 min)
  • Resuspend the Oligo(dT)25 beads fully, then pipette 20 µL per sample into a fresh tube and place it on the magnet for 2 min until clear [2][3]. (5 min)
  • Remove the supernatant, add 100 µL binding buffer, pipette 6 times, magnetize 2 min and discard. Repeat for two washes in total [2][3]. (6 min)
  • Resuspend the washed beads in 50 µL binding buffer and add the 50 µL denatured RNA (100 µL total); pipette gently to mix [2][3]. (2 min)
  • Bind by rotating or resuspending at room temperature for 5–10 min total; longer binding gives little extra yield for total RNA [1][3][4]. (10 min)
  • Magnetize for 2 min and discard the supernatant, keeping it at −20 °C until the yield is confirmed in case the capture needs repeating [2][3]. (3 min)
  • Wash twice with 200 µL wash buffer (resuspend, magnetize 2 min, discard); for crude lysate add one wash with 0.1% LiDS wash buffer first [2][3][4]. (8 min)
  • Remove all residual wash buffer with a 10 µL tip after a brief spin; carryover salt or LiDS inhibits reverse transcriptase [2][4]. (2 min)
  • O (p)
  • To elute, add 50 µL 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 (or nuclease-free water) and heat at 80 °C for 2 min [2][3]. (3 min)
  • Magnetize at once while still warm and transfer the supernatant to a fresh RNase-free tube; cooling first lets poly(A) RNA rebind the beads [2][3]. (2 min)
  • For a second round, add the eluate to an equal volume of binding buffer and heat 65 °C for 5 min, then snap-cool on ice [3]. (8 min)
  • Wash the used beads twice with 200 µL wash buffer, add the diluted eluate, and bind 5 min at room temperature, then magnetize and discard [3]. (10 min)
  • Wash the rebound beads twice with 200 µL wash buffer and remove all residual liquid before elution [2][3]. (5 min)
  • Elute the second round in 17–20 µL 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0 at 80 °C for 2 min, magnetize at once and collect the supernatant [2][3]. (4 min)
  • Quantify 1–2 µL on a NanoDrop (expect only 1–5% of input RNA; A260/280 about 2.0) or a fluorometric RNA assay [1][2]. (5 min)
  • Check 1 µL on a Bioanalyzer RNA Pico chip: rRNA peaks (18S/28S) should be largely gone and a smear of 0.5–4 kb remain [2]. (30 min)
  • Use the mRNA immediately for library prep or RT, or store at −80 °C in small aliquots; avoid repeated freeze–thaw [1]. (5 min)
  • To reuse beads on a different sample, treat with 0.1 N NaOH for 5 min, wash 3 times with water or Tris, then store in PBS with 0.1% Tween 20 and 0.05% NaN₃ [1]. (20 min)
  • Total RNA can be stored at −80 °C before the purification.
  • Purified mRNA in elution buffer can be stored at −80 °C; avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles.
  • Do not pause between binding and washing, because poly(A) RNA–bead complexes should be processed promptly.
  • Typical yield is about 1–5% of input total RNA by mass (for example 20–100 ng from 2 µg), measured on a NanoDrop or fluorometer.
  • Bioanalyzer trace shows little 18S/28S rRNA and a broad 0.5–4 kb mRNA smear; a single round leaves a few percent rRNA, two rounds much less.
  • Input total RNA aliquot run on the Bioanalyzer to confirm RIN and compare rRNA peaks
  • Flow-through (unbound fraction) kept to confirm rRNA stayed unbound and mRNA was captured
  • No-RNA bead blank taken through elution to check reagent contamination
  • RT-qPCR of a poly(A) transcript (such as ACTB) against 18S rRNA in input versus eluate
ProblemLikely causeSolution
Low mRNA yieldDegraded RNA, too few beads, or skipped denaturationCheck RIN, scale beads to input, repeat the 65 °C denaturation and snap-cool
High rRNA carryoverSingle round only or insufficient washingPerform the second binding round and wash fully with 0.15 M LiCl buffer
Poor performance in RT or library prepResidual wash buffer, LiDS or ethanolRemove all wash buffer with a fine tip; rewash with enzyme-compatible buffer
Beads lost or clumpedDrying, freezing, or aspiration near the pelletKeep beads moist, pipette away from the pellet, resuspend fully
Degraded mRNA after elutionRNase contamination or heating too longUse RNase-free consumables, limit 80 °C to 2 min, add RNase inhibitor
Viscous lysate (crude samples)Genomic DNA releaseShear by passing through a 21-gauge needle several times
  • LiDS, DTT and NaN₃ are toxic or irritating: wear gloves and eye protection and dispose of waste per local rules.
  • Sodium azide can form explosive metal azides in drains; collect it as hazardous waste.
  • Handle hot blocks and tubes at 65–80 °C with care.
  • Treat human or animal samples as potentially infectious and follow local biosafety rules.

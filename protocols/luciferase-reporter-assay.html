Luciferase Reporter Assay

Estimated time: 2–3 days | Protocol World

To measure the activity of a promoter, enhancer or signaling pathway in cultured mammalian cells (for example HEK293T) by quantifying firefly luciferase from a test reporter and normalizing it to a co-transfected Renilla luciferase control (dual-luciferase format).

A test construct places firefly luciferase under control of the regulatory element of interest, while a second plasmid drives constitutive Renilla luciferase. Cell lysate is first mixed with a reagent that gives firefly luminescence; a second reagent then quenches the firefly signal and supplies the substrate for Renilla, so both activities are read from the same sample [1]. Dividing firefly by Renilla signal per well corrects for differences in cell number and transfection efficiency [1][2][3]. The signal is read in a luminometer; the assay is sensitive and spans several orders of magnitude [1].

ItemDetails
Cells and complete mediumHEK293T (or your cell line) in DMEM with 10% serum; cells should be at most 95% confluent at lysis [1]
Firefly reporter plasmidTest promoter or response element upstream of firefly luciferase (for example pGL3-based) [3]
Renilla control plasmidFor example pRL-TK; keep the reporter:Renilla ratio between 10:1 and 50:1 by mass to avoid trans effects [1][3]
Transfection reagent and serum-free mediumLipid-based reagent suited to your cells; follow the manufacturer's DNA:reagent ratios
Control constructsEmpty reporter vector (no promoter or response element) and, if available, a positive-control construct or stimulus
1× PBSSterile, room temperature
1× Passive Lysis Buffer (PLB)Dilute 5× PLB 1:5 with distilled water (1 volume + 4 volumes); fresh is best, 1× is stable about 1 month at 4 °C [1]
Luciferase Assay Reagent II (LAR II)Lyophilized substrate reconstituted in the supplied buffer; aliquot and store at −20 °C (1 month) or −70 °C (1 year); warm to room temperature before use [1]
Stop & Glo ReagentSubstrate 1:50 in Stop & Glo Buffer (200 µL per 10 mL) for the Dual-Luciferase kit; make fresh at room temperature [1]
White opaque assay plates or tubesOpaque plates reduce cross-talk between wells [1]
  • Biosafety cabinet
  • 37 °C incubator, 5% CO₂
  • Plate rocker or orbital shaker
  • Luminometer (single-tube or plate reader; avoid scintillation counters)
  • Multichannel or repeater pipette
  • Cell counter
  • Plate cells so they are 60–70% confluent at transfection [3]. As a starting point, about 1 × 10⁵ HEK293T cells per well of a 24-well plate; adjust for your line. (Day 1)
  • Transfect triplicate wells with reporter plus Renilla plasmid at 10:1–50:1 by mass (Bio-protocol: 2 µg:200 ng per 6-well well). Scale to the well size [1][3]. (30 min)
  • Add any effector or expression plasmid and the transfection reagent as the manufacturer directs, and add complexes to the cells [3]. (20 min)
  • Incubate cells at 37 °C, 5% CO₂ for the expression period (48 h in the Bio-protocol example); apply any drug or stimulus for the last hours as required [3]. (24–48 h)
  • Remove the medium and gently wash the cells once with PBS; remove all of the rinse [1][3]. (2 min)
  • Add 1× PLB to cover the cells (24-well: 100 µL; 6-well: 500 µL; 96-well: 20 µL) and rock at room temperature for 15 min [1][3]. (15 min)
  • Equilibrate LAR II and Stop & Glo to room temperature. Dispense 100 µL LAR II per tube or well of the white plate [1][3]. (10 min)
  • Transfer up to 20 µL lysate to the LAR II and mix by pipetting 2–3 times; do not vortex the lysate [1]. (1 min)
  • Read firefly luminescence with a 2 s pre-read delay and a 10 s measurement [1][3]. (12 s)
  • Add 100 µL Stop & Glo, mix, and read Renilla luminescence the same way (2 s delay, 10 s measurement) [1][3]. (12 s)
  • Include blank wells (untransfected-cell lysate) to measure background for both readings [1]. (as needed)
  • Calculate firefly ÷ Renilla for each well, average technical replicates, then divide by the ratio of the control condition (for example the empty vector) to give fold change [1][2][3]. (15 min)
  • Lysates can be kept for 6 h at room temperature (22 °C), up to 16 h on ice, 1 month at −20 °C and longer at −70 °C; avoid more than 2–3 freeze-thaws [1].
  • Reconstituted LAR II can be frozen in aliquots and Stop & Glo is best made fresh [1].
  • Firefly and Renilla signals well above the blank (background); the empty-vector ratio gives the basal level.
  • A promoter or response element that is active increases the firefly/Renilla ratio relative to the empty vector, and a pathway activator or inhibitor shifts it further.
  • The assay is linear over many orders of magnitude [1]; if readings exceed the luminometer range, dilute the lysate.
  • Empty reporter vector (no regulatory element) to define basal activity
  • Untransfected-cell lysate to measure firefly and Renilla background [1]
  • A positive control (a strongly active promoter or a known pathway stimulus) to confirm the assay works
  • Renilla co-transfected in every well to normalize for cell number and transfection efficiency [1][2][3]
  • Identical volumes and medium in background wells and samples [2]
ProblemLikely causeSolution
Low signalsPoor transfection, low DNA quality, over-confluent cells or too little lysateCheck transfection, use endotoxin-free DNA, keep confluence under 95%, optimize DNA amount [1]
High well-to-well variationPipetting error or uneven transfectionUse master mixes, normalize to Renilla, increase replicates [1][3]
Firefly signal falls as Renilla risesStrong trans effect from a too-active Renilla plasmidRaise the reporter:Renilla ratio toward 50:1 or switch to a weaker control promoter [1][2]
Firefly carryover in Renilla readingResidual firefly signal after Stop & GloMatters only if Renilla is more than about 1,000-fold lower; ensure Stop & Glo is fresh [1]
Cross-talk between wellsClear or white-sided platesUse opaque white plates [1]
Signal drifts during a plate readFlash kinetics decay quicklyRead samples at consistent times, or use the Dual-Glo format, which is stable for about 2 h [2]
  • The lyophilized luciferase substrate contains dithiothreitol (DTT) and the Stop & Glo substrate contains a volatile solvent: wear gloves, a lab coat and eye protection, and keep caps closed [2].
  • Handle HEK293T and other human cells at biosafety level 2 in a cabinet; bleach or autoclave all liquid and solid waste that touched cells.
  • Dispose of used luciferase reagents as chemical waste according to your institution's policy.

C. elegans Germline Dissection

Estimated time: 2.5 h hands-on + overnight incubations | Protocol World

To extrude intact gonads from adult C. elegans hermaphrodites so that germline nuclei, chromosomes and proteins can be stained with DAPI or antibodies and imaged without the cuticle and intestine in the way.

The two U-shaped gonad arms lie coiled inside the worm. Cutting an anesthetized adult at the pharynx or tail releases the internal pressure, and one or both gonad arms usually push out through the cut. The extruded germline is then fixed (formaldehyde, methanol or both), stained and mounted flat for imaging [1][2][3]. Levamisole paralyzes the worms but alters germline morphology if left on for too long, so dissections must be finished promptly [2]. Fixation choice matters: methanol alone is quick but gonads break easily, whereas a brief formaldehyde step followed by cold methanol preserves morphology better for many antibodies [1].

ItemDetails
Young adult hermaphrodites30–60 animals per tube, from a well-fed, uncrowded plate; the sources do not specify hours after L4, so keep the staging consistent between experiments [1][2][3]
PBS1× phosphate-buffered saline, pH 7.4 (home-made or commercial)
Levamisole stock100 mM in water, stored at −20 °C; dilute 1:500 to 0.2 mM in PBS for the dissection dish [1]. Tetramisole (0.5 mM with 0.05% Tween 20 in M9) was used in a published variant [3]
PBTPBS + 0.1% Tween 20 (v/v) [1]
Formaldehyde fixative3% formaldehyde in 0.1 M K₂HPO₄, pH 7.2, made from EM-grade 16% formaldehyde [1]
Methanol100%, pre-chilled to −20 °C
Antibody diluentPBT + 30% goat serum; use 1 mg/mL BSA in PBT instead if the primary antibody was raised in goat [1]
Primary and secondary antibodiesDilutions must be determined empirically for each antibody [1]
DAPI100 µg/mL stock in water (−20 °C); working solution 100 ng/mL in PBT (1:1,000) [1]
DABCO mounting medium1% DABCO in 90% glycerol in PBS, stored at −20 °C [1]; commercial antifade with DAPI is an alternative [3]
Agar pads2% agar (or agarose) on glass slides, large enough for a 24 × 50 mm coverslip [1]
Nail polish or rubber cementTo seal the coverslip for storage
  • Stereomicroscope with transmitted light
  • Two 22–25 G syringe needles with straight, unbent tips [1][3][4]
  • Flat-bottomed glass dish (for example, Carolina Biological Supply dissection dish) [1]
  • Drawn-out glass Pasteur pipettes
  • Small glass or siliconized tubes (about 6 mm × 35 mm) and a benchtop centrifuge [1]
  • −20 °C freezer
  • Glass slides and 24 × 50 mm coverslips
  • Epifluorescence or confocal microscope with a DAPI filter set
  • Prepare 0.2 mM levamisole in PBS (2 µL of 100 mM stock per mL), PBT, and 100% methanol chilled to −20 °C. Check that needle tips are straight [1]. (15 min)
  • Pick 30–60 young adults onto an unseeded NGM plate to shed bacteria, then place them in 1–3 mL levamisole/PBS in a flat-bottomed glass dish [1][2][3]. (10 min)
  • Under the stereomicroscope, cross two needles at the level of the pharynx and cut off the head with a scissor-like motion. At least one gonad arm should extrude [1][2][4]. (1–2 min per worm)
  • Dissect as many animals as possible in 5–15 min; levamisole changes germline morphology after about 15 min. Discard animals whose gonads did not extrude [1][2]. (5–15 min)
  • With a drawn-out Pasteur pipette, move the worms and gonads into a small glass tube and let them settle; pellet gently (1 min, low speed) and remove most of the liquid [1]. (5 min)
  • Fix: add 2 mL of 3% formaldehyde in 0.1 M K₂HPO₄ (pH 7.2) for 10 min; pellet 1 min and wash once in PBT [1]. For quick DAPI-only work, methanol alone for 5 min can be used [1]. (15 min)
  • Post-fix in 2 mL cold (−20 °C) methanol for 5 min, then pellet and wash twice in PBT. Gonads can be stored in methanol at −20 °C for a few days [1]. (15 min)
  • Leave about 100 µL liquid. Add primary antibody diluted in PBT + 30% goat serum (50–100 µL total) and incubate 8 h to overnight; use 4 °C for overnight incubations [1]. (8 h–overnight)
  • Pellet 1 min and wash 4 × 5 min with 500 µL PBT [1]. (25 min)
  • Add fluorescent secondary antibody in PBT + 30% goat serum (50–100 µL) and incubate 4 h to overnight in the dark [1]. (4 h–overnight)
  • Wash 3 × 5 min with 500 µL PBT [1]. (20 min)
  • Stain with 100 ng/mL DAPI in PBT for 5 min, then rinse twice in PBT and add DABCO anti-fade [1]. (10 min)
  • Transfer worms and gonads with a drawn capillary onto a 2% agar pad, remove excess liquid, cover with a 24 × 50 mm coverslip and do not move it [1]. (15 min)
  • Leave unsealed so gonads flatten as liquid evaporates (overnight is typical), then seal with nail polish and store at 4 °C; image with 40× or 63× objectives [1]. (Overnight)
  • After methanol post-fixation the gonads can be stored at −20 °C for a few days [1].
  • After the primary antibody incubation, wash and continue the next day.
  • Sealed slides can be stored at 4 °C for a week or more [1].
  • Intact gonad arms with distal mitotic zone, transition zone, pachytene region and developing oocytes visible as a continuous strip.
  • Crisp DAPI-stained nuclei; meiotic chromosome morphology is resolved in pachytene nuclei.
  • Specific antibody signal in the germline with little signal in the surrounding extruded tissue.
  • No-primary-antibody control to check secondary background
  • Wild-type (N2) gonads stained in parallel with mutant or transgenic gonads
  • Known-pattern antibody (a germline marker with a well-described pattern) as a positive control
  • Mutant lacking the antigen, if available
ProblemLikely causeSolution
No gonads extrudeCut too far from the head, blunt or bent needles, old animalsCut at the pharynx with fresh straight needles; use young adults
Gonads break during washesMethanol-only fixation; harsh pipettingUse the formaldehyde plus methanol fixation; use wide-bore or drawn pipettes and low-speed spins
Distorted germline morphologyToo long in levamisoleFinish dissections within about 15 min of adding levamisole
High backgroundInsufficient washing or blockingAdd a blocking step or use BSA in PBT; use goat-free diluent with goat primary antibodies
Weak signalAntibody not optimized; epitope sensitive to fixativeTitrate the antibody; compare 10 min and longer formaldehyde fixation or methanol only
Gonads lost on the slideOverflowing liquid or moving the coverslipRemove excess liquid with a capillary and do not move the coverslip after placing it
  • Formaldehyde is toxic and a suspected carcinogen: handle in a fume hood with gloves and eye protection.
  • Methanol is flammable and toxic; store and use away from ignition sources.
  • Levamisole is toxic if swallowed; avoid skin contact.
  • Needles are sharp: dispose of in a sharps container.
  • Collect formaldehyde and methanol waste in labeled hazardous-waste containers; follow institutional rules.

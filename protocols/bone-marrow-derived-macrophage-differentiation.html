Bone Marrow-Derived Macrophage Differentiation

≈ 8 days · 2026-11-27

Differentiate mouse bone marrow progenitors into macrophages over 7 days using L929-conditioned medium: marrow harvest, ACK lysis, seeding on non-TC plastic, feeding, EDTA detachment, replating, purity check and troubleshooting.

⬇ Word (.docx)⬇ PowerPoint (.pptx)

Purpose

To generate a large, uniform population of primary mouse macrophages (BMDMs) by culturing bone marrow progenitors for 7 days with macrophage colony-stimulating factor (M-CSF) supplied as L929-conditioned medium.

Principle

Bone marrow contains myeloid progenitors that differentiate into macrophages when M-CSF is present. L929 fibroblasts secrete M-CSF into their culture medium, so a defined percentage of L929-conditioned medium (LCM) can replace recombinant cytokine [1][2][3]. Cells are grown on non-tissue-culture-treated plastic so that mature macrophages adhere only loosely and can be lifted gently with cold EDTA, rather than by trypsin or scraping [2][3]. By day 6–7 the culture is a near-uniform CD11b⁺ F4/80⁺ macrophage population ready for stimulation or assays [1][2]. Settings vary between laboratories (10–30% conditioned medium, 6–7 days); this protocol follows the best-documented numerical version [3] and notes alternatives [1][2].

Materials and reagents

ItemDetails
MiceC57BL/6 or other strain, 8–10 weeks old, either sex; euthanize by an approved method (CO₂ followed by cervical dislocation) [1][3]
L929 mediumDMEM high glucose with sodium pyruvate, 10% FBS, 2 mM L-glutamine, penicillin–streptomycin, 10 mM HEPES [3]
Base mediumDMEM high glucose with sodium pyruvate, 10% FBS, 2 mM L-glutamine, penicillin–streptomycin (β-mercaptoethanol optional) [3]
L929-conditioned medium (LCM)Prepared in advance (steps 1–4), filtered and stored at −20 °C
Complete BMDM mediumBase medium + 20% LCM (v/v) [3]; other labs use 10% [1] or 30% [2]
70% ethanol and sterile PBSFor disinfecting bones and washing
ACK lysis buffer2 mL per mouse to lyse red blood cells [3]
PBS + 2.5 mM EDTAIce-cold, for detaching macrophages on day 7 [3]
Cells and plasticsL929 cells; non-tissue-culture-treated dishes or plates (essential); T75 flasks; 70 µm cell strainers; 5 mL syringes with 18 G and 27 G needles; 0.2 µm PES filter

Equipment

  • Biosafety cabinet
  • 37 °C, 5% CO₂ incubator
  • Refrigerated centrifuge (250–350 × g)
  • Dissection tools (sterile scissors, forceps)
  • Cell counter or hemocytometer
  • Flow cytometer (optional, for purity check)

Procedure

  1. Thaw L929 cells into 10 mL L929 medium, spin at 250 × g for 8 min, and resuspend in 10 mL [3]. (20 min)
  2. Seed 4.7 × 10⁵ L929 cells in 55 mL L929 medium in a T75 flask, one flask per 50 mL of LCM wanted [1][3]. (10 min)
  3. Culture at 37 °C, 5% CO₂ for 7 days without changing the medium [1][3]. (7 days)
  4. Collect the medium, filter through a 0.2 µm PES filter (0.45 µm is also used [1]), and store 10 mL aliquots at −20 °C. A flask yields about 50 mL [3]. (30 min)
  5. Day 0: euthanize mice, spray with 70% ethanol, remove the hind legs above the hip joint and strip off muscle [1][3]. (20 min)
  6. Wash bones 30 s in 70% ethanol and 30 s in PBS, then keep them in 10 mL cold base medium per mouse [3].
  7. Clip both ends of each femur and tibia. Flush the marrow with base medium using a 27 G needle on a 5 mL syringe until the bone looks white [1][3]. (20 min)
  8. Break up clumps with a syringe plunger and pass cells through a 70 µm strainer, rinsing with about 5 mL medium [1][3].
  9. Pellet at 350 × g for 8 min at 4 °C. Resuspend in 2 mL ACK buffer, mix 1 min at room temperature, then dilute with 10 mL base medium [3]. (15 min)
  10. Pellet again (350 × g, 8 min, 4 °C), resuspend in about 5 mL base medium, filter through a 70 µm strainer and count. Expect 15–45 × 10⁶ cells per mouse [3].
  11. Dilute cells in complete BMDM medium and seed 4 × 10⁴ cells/cm² on non-TC-treated plastic, for example 5.9 × 10⁶ cells in 30 mL per 150 mm dish [3]. (20 min)
  12. Incubate at 37 °C, 5% CO₂. Day 3: add complete medium equal to two-thirds of the starting volume (20 mL for a 150 mm dish) without removing the old medium [3]. (3 days)
  13. Day 6–7: check the culture. Adherent, spread macrophages should cover the dish; some labs feed again with fresh medium every 2–3 days [1][2]. (day 6–7)
  14. Day 7: remove medium, rinse twice with PBS, add PBS + 2.5 mM EDTA and chill at 4 °C for 30 min [3]. (30 min)
  15. Detach by vigorous pipetting (no scraping), collect, rinse the dish, pellet cells at 200–450 × g for 5–10 min and count [1][3].
  16. Replate for experiments, for example 1.56 × 10⁵ cells/cm² (3 × 10⁵ cells in 0.75 mL per 24-well), and rest at least 8 h before treatment [3].
  17. Optional purity check: stain 5 × 10⁵ cells with Fc block and anti-CD11b and anti-F4/80 antibodies (about 30 min, 4 °C). More than 90% CD11b⁺ is the benchmark [1][2]. (1 h)

Pause points

  • The L929-conditioned medium can be stored at −20 °C for months before use.
  • Cultures can stay in the incubator between day 3 and day 7 without handling.
  • After detachment, rested BMDMs can be held for experiments for up to about 3 weeks in culture [1].

Expected results

  • 15–45 × 10⁶ marrow cells per mouse at harvest; recovery on day 7 close to the seeding density of about 4 × 10⁴ cells/cm² [3].
  • Adherent, spread macrophages by day 6–7; flow cytometry shows a double-positive CD11b⁺ F4/80⁺ population, above 90% CD11b⁺ [1][2].

Controls

  • Undifferentiated marrow cells stained for CD11b and F4/80 as a negative gating control
  • Unstained and single-stain flow controls
  • A culture without LCM: cells should not expand or adhere
  • Test each new LCM batch on a small culture before large-scale use

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely causeSolution
Few adherent cells at day 7Low LCM potency or too few cells seededTest a new LCM batch; raise LCM to 30% [2]; seed at the stated density
Cells will not detachTissue-culture-treated plastic or warm bufferUse non-TC plastic; chill with EDTA at 4 °C for 30 min; pipette vigorously [3]
Clumps and low yield at harvestMarrow not fully flushed or not filteredClip bone ends, flush until white, use a 70 µm strainer [3]
Low purity by flow cytometryContaminating lymphocytes or weak differentiationExtend culture to day 7, confirm LCM batch, wash away non-adherent cells at feeding [1]
ContaminationBones not disinfected or non-sterile reagentsRepeat the ethanol and PBS washes; use sterile instruments and filtered LCM

Safety and waste

  • Follow your institution's animal-use approval for euthanasia and tissue handling.
  • Use sharps (needles, scissors) with care and dispose of them in a sharps container.
  • Treat primary cells and animal tissue as potentially biohazardous; decontaminate waste with bleach or autoclave.
  • Wear gloves, lab coat and eye protection; ACK buffer and ethanol waste go to the appropriate chemical stream.

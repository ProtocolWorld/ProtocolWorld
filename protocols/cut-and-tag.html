CUT&Tag

General Lab Methods · 2 days (about 8 h hands-on) · 2026-11-26

Bind up to 500,000 cells to ConA beads, permeabilize with digitonin, tether pA-Tn5 with primary and secondary antibodies, tagment at 37 °C with Mg²⁺, extract DNA, amplify 13 PCR cycles and check the library for sequencing.

CUT&Tag

Estimated time: 2 days (about 8 h hands-on) | Protocol World

To map histone modifications or chromatin-associated proteins genome-wide from as few as tens of thousands of mammalian cells by antibody-guided tagmentation, producing sequencing libraries directly in the tube.

Cells or nuclei are bound to concanavalin A (ConA) magnetic beads and permeabilized with digitonin. A primary antibody binds the target, a secondary antibody bridges to protein A fused to Tn5 transposase (pA-Tn5), and the enzyme is held at the target chromatin. Adding Mg²⁺ activates Tn5, which cuts the DNA next to the antibody and inserts sequencing adapters in one step. The tagged fragments are released, amplified by limited-cycle PCR with indexed primers and sequenced [1][2]. Because the background is low, 2–3 million read pairs are usually enough for histone marks and transcription factors [1].

ItemDetails
Mammalian cellsUp to about 500,000 cells per sample in the bench-top protocol (K562 or H1 ES cells were used); fresh, viable cells are preferred [1][3]
ConA magnetic beads10 µL slurry per sample (Bangs Laboratories BP531 in [1]); wash twice in Binding buffer before use
Binding buffer20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.9, 10 mM KCl, 1 mM CaCl₂, 1 mM MnCl₂; 4 °C [1]
Wash buffer20 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 0.5 mM spermidine, plus one EDTA-free protease-inhibitor tablet per 50 mL; use within 1 week at 4 °C [1]
Dig-washWash buffer + 0.05% digitonin (400 µL of 5% digitonin per 40 mL); 4 °C, up to 2 days [1]
Antibody bufferDig-wash + 2 mM EDTA + about 0.1% BSA, kept on ice [1]
Dig-30020 mM HEPES pH 7.5, 300 mM NaCl, 0.5 mM spermidine, protease inhibitor, 0.01% digitonin; add digitonin the same day [1]
Tagmentation bufferDig-300 + 10 mM MgCl₂ [1]
Digitonin 5% stock50 mg in 1 mL DMSO; commercial solutions may need warming to 65 °C for 5 min before use [1][4]
Primary antibodyTypically 1:50–1:100 (0.5–1 µL per sample); positive control H3K27me3 (Cell Signaling 9733 used in [1]); IgG negative control [1]
Secondary antibodySpecies-matched to the primary (for example, guinea pig anti-rabbit IgG); 1:100 in Dig-wash [1]
pA-Tn5 adapter complexLoaded with ME-A/ME-B annealed to ME-reverse; used at 1:250 in Dig-300 (from 5.5 µM pA-Tn5 loaded as in [1]); commercial pAG-Tn5 is an alternative [3][4]
Stop and digestion reagents0.5 M EDTA, 10% SDS, 20 mg/mL Proteinase K [1]
DNA extraction reagentsPhenol–chloroform–isoamyl alcohol, chloroform, 100% and 80% ethanol; or SPRI beads for the on-bead release variants [1][3][4]
PCR reagentsNEBNext HiFi 2× PCR master mix; 10 µM universal i5 and barcoded i7 primers (not Nextera primers) [1]
SPRI beadsFor post-PCR cleanup (1.3× in [1])
Elution and resuspension buffers1 mM Tris-HCl pH 8, 0.1 mM EDTA; 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8
  • Magnet stand for 1.5 mL or 0.2 mL tubes
  • End-over-end rotator or nutator
  • Thermomixer or heat block (37 °C and 55 °C)
  • Thermocycler with heated lid
  • Benchtop centrifuge, 16,000 × g capable
  • Vortex mixer
  • TapeStation, Bioanalyzer or equivalent for library QC
  • Qubit fluorometer
  • Illumina sequencer (paired-end)
  • Prepare all buffers; keep Antibody buffer and Dig-wash on ice. Wash 10 µL ConA beads per sample twice in Binding buffer and resuspend in the original volume [1]. (30 min)
  • Harvest up to 500,000 cells per sample, wash, and resuspend in 1 mL Wash buffer. Add beads while gently vortexing and rotate 5–10 min at room temperature [1]. (20 min)
  • Place tubes on a magnet, discard the supernatant, and resuspend in 50 µL cold Antibody buffer. Add 0.5–1 µL primary antibody (1:50–1:100) [1]. (10 min)
  • Incubate with primary antibody for 2 h at room temperature, or overnight at 4 °C on a nutator [1]. (2 h–overnight)
  • Remove liquid on the magnet. Add 100 µL secondary antibody at 1:100 in Dig-wash and nutate 30–60 min at room temperature [1]. (45 min)
  • Wash 3 × with 1 mL Dig-wash on the magnet to remove unbound secondary antibody [1]. (15 min)
  • Add 100 µL pA-Tn5 at 1:250 in Dig-300 and nutate 1 h at room temperature [1]. (1 h)
  • Wash 3 × with 1 mL Dig-300 to remove unbound pA-Tn5 [1]. (15 min)
  • Resuspend in 300 µL Tagmentation buffer (Dig-300 + 10 mM MgCl₂) and incubate 1 h at 37 °C [1]. (1 h)
  • Stop: add 10 µL 0.5 M EDTA, 3 µL 10% SDS and 2.5 µL 20 mg/mL Proteinase K; vortex 2 s and incubate 1 h at 55 °C [1]. (1 h)
  • Add 300 µL phenol–chloroform–isoamyl alcohol, transfer to a phase-lock tube, spin 3 min at 16,000 × g; add 300 µL chloroform and spin again [1]. (15 min)
  • Move the aqueous layer to 750 µL 100% ethanol, chill at least 10 min at 4 °C, spin at 16,000 × g, rinse in 1 mL ethanol, dry briefly, and dissolve in 25–30 µL 1 mM Tris-HCl/0.1 mM EDTA [1]. (40 min)
  • PCR: 21 µL DNA, 2 µL i5, 2 µL i7 and 25 µL NEBNext HiFi 2×. Cycle: 72 °C 5 min; 98 °C 30 s; 13 × (98 °C 10 s, 63 °C 10 s); 72 °C 1 min; hold at 8 °C [1]. (45 min)
  • Clean up with 1.3× SPRI beads (65 µL), wash twice with 80% ethanol on the magnet without over-drying, and elute in 25 µL 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8 [1]. (30 min)
  • Check the library on a TapeStation D1000: look for a mononucleosome peak near 350 bp with oligonucleosome bands above it, then pool equal amounts for paired-end sequencing [1]. (1 h)
  • After overnight primary antibody incubation at 4 °C, continue with the secondary antibody the next morning [1].
  • The DNA after phenol–chloroform extraction can be stored at −20 °C before PCR.
  • Finished libraries can be stored at −20 °C until pooling and sequencing.
  • A TapeStation trace with a mononucleosome-sized peak near 350 bp and larger nucleosome-ladder bands for H3K27me3; no ladder means the experiment failed [1].
  • Sharp peaks over known domains (broad H3K27me3 domains; narrow H3K4me3 peaks at promoters) in a genome browser.
  • About 2–3 million read pairs are enough for histone marks and transcription factors; one example reached an 80% fraction of reads in peaks with roughly 800,000 read pairs for H3K4me3 [1].
  • Positive control antibody: H3K27me3 (abundant, forms a nucleosome ladder) [1]
  • Negative control: IgG, or omit the primary antibody [1][3]
  • Replicates from independent cell preparations
  • E. coli carry-over DNA from the pA-Tn5 prep can serve as a spike-in for normalization [1]

  • Phenol and chloroform are toxic and corrosive: use only in a fume hood with nitrile or butyl gloves and eye protection.
  • Digitonin is toxic if swallowed or inhaled; weigh in a hood and avoid dust.
  • DMSO carries dissolved chemicals through skin; wear gloves.
  • Treat human cell lines as biohazards per biosafety rules.
  • Dispose of phenol–chloroform and ethanol waste in labeled hazardous-waste containers.

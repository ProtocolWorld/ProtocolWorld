Ribosome Profiling

5–7 days for the library + 4–5 days for sequencing and analysis · 2026-11-27

Map translating ribosomes in cultured mammalian cells: cycloheximide lysis, RNase I footprinting, sucrose cushion, 26–34 nt size selection, linker ligation, circularization, rRNA subtraction and PCR for Illumina sequencing.

⬇ Word (.docx)⬇ PowerPoint (.pptx)

Purpose

To map translating ribosomes across the transcriptome of cultured mammalian cells by sequencing the ~30 nt mRNA fragments protected by ribosomes (ribosome footprints), giving genome-wide translation levels and codon-level positions.

Principle

Elongating ribosomes are frozen on mRNA with cycloheximide, the lysate is digested with RNase I so that only ribosome-protected fragments survive, and the monosomes are pelleted through a sucrose cushion. Footprints are size-selected, dephosphorylated, ligated to a 3′ linker, reverse transcribed, circularized, depleted of rRNA fragments and amplified for Illumina sequencing [1][2]. Reads show characteristic fragment lengths and reading-frame periodicity if the digestion worked [1].

Equipment

  • Ultracentrifuge, TLA100.3 rotor
  • Microcentrifuge, 20,000 × g
  • 26G needles, syringes
  • Gel units, blue-light box
  • Thermal cycler, thermomixer
  • Bioanalyzer and fluorometer
  • Illumina sequencer (core)
  • −80 °C freezer, dry ice

Procedure

  1. Prepare polysome buffer with DTT and cycloheximide fresh; add Triton X-100 and Turbo DNase I for lysis buffer and keep on ice [2]. (20 min)
  2. Place the 10 cm dish of growing cells on ice, aspirate the medium and wash with 10 mL ice-cold PBS; remove all liquid [2]. (3 min)
  3. Add 400 µL ice-cold lysis buffer, scrape, collect the lysate and keep it on ice for 10 min [2]. (10 min)
  4. Pass the lysate through a 26G needle 10 times, then clarify at 20,000 × g for 10 min at 4 °C; keep the supernatant [2]. (15 min)
  5. Mix 300 µL clarified lysate with 7.5 µL RNase I (100 U/µL) and incubate 45 min at room temperature with gentle mixing [2]. (45 min)
  6. Stop the digestion with 10 µL SUPERase-In (20 U/µL) and place on ice [2]. (5 min)
  7. Layer the digest over 0.90 mL of 1 M sucrose cushion in a 13 × 51 mm polycarbonate tube [2]. (10 min)
  8. Spin in a TLA100.3 rotor at 70,000 rpm for 4 h at 4 °C. Mark the tube edge to find the glassy translucent pellet [2]. (4 h)
  9. Discard the supernatant, resuspend the pellet in 700 µL QIAzol and purify total RNA with a miRNeasy column with small RNA retained [2]. (1 h)
  10. Add 1.5 µL GlycoBlue, precipitate the RNA and resuspend in 5 µL 10 mM Tris pH 8 [2]. (1 h)
  11. Run 15% TBE-urea gel (pre-run 15 min, then 65 min at 200 V) with RNA and the 26 nt and 34 nt markers in a separate lane [1][2][4]. (2 h)
  12. Stain with SYBR Gold 1× for 3 min and view on a blue-light transilluminator (UV damages RNA); excise the 26–34 nt window [1][2]. (30 min)
  13. Elute the RNA from the crushed gel (e.g., 300 mM NaOAc pH 5.5, 1 mM EDTA, 0.25% SDS overnight) and precipitate with GlycoBlue [1]. (overnight)
  14. Dephosphorylate with 10 U T4 PNK, 1 µL SUPERase-In and 10× PNK buffer (no ATP) in 50 µL for 1 h at 37 °C; heat-kill 10 min at 70 °C [2]. (1.5 h)
  15. Precipitate the RNA with GlycoBlue and resuspend it in about 8.5 µL water for ligation [2]. (1 h)
  16. Mix 8.5 µL RNA with 1.5 µL linker (0.5 µg/µL), denature 90 s at 80 °C, then add 10× ligase buffer, 6 µL 50% PEG 8000, SUPERase-In and ligase; 20 µL [2]. (10 min)
  17. Incubate the ligation for 2.5 h at room temperature, then dilute with 338 µL water so PEG does not impair the pellet; precipitate [2]. (3 h)
  18. Run on a 15% TBE-urea gel and excise the ligated product (footprint plus linker), leaving unligated linker behind; elute and precipitate [1][2]. (overnight)
  19. Anneal 2 µL of 1.25 µM RT primer by heating 2 min at 80 °C then cooling to 48 °C; add buffer, dNTPs, DTT, SUPERase-In and RT (20 µL total) [2]. (10 min)
  20. Run reverse transcription for 30 min at 48 °C; add 2.2 µL 1 N NaOH and hydrolyze RNA for 20 min at 98 °C [1][2]. (1 h)
  21. Gel-purify the cDNA on a 15% denaturing gel with a primer-only lane; cut the extended product above the unextended primer, elute and precipitate [1][2]. (overnight)
  22. Circularize 15 µL cDNA with 2 µL 10× CircLigase buffer, 1 µL 1 mM ATP, 1 µL 50 mM MnCl₂ and 1 µL CircLigase for 1 h at 60 °C; inactivate 10 min at 80 °C [2]. (1.5 h)
  23. Subtract rRNA: 5 µL circularized DNA + 1 µL biotinylated oligo mix + 1 µL 20× SSC + 3 µL water; 90 s at 100 °C, cool at 0.1 °C/s to 37 °C, hold 15 min [2]. (1 h)
  24. Add the reaction to washed MyOne C1 beads (250 µg in 10 µL 2× bind/wash); mix 15 min at 37 °C, keep the supernatant (17.5 µL) and precipitate [2]. (1 h)
  25. Set up a 100 µL PCR: 5 µL DNA, 5× Phusion HF buffer, 2 µL 10 mM dNTP, forward and indexed reverse primers, 1 µL Phusion (2 U/µL); aliquot 16.7 µL into 5 tubes [2]. (15 min)
  26. Cycle: 98 °C 30 s; then 98 °C 10 s, 65 °C 10 s, 72 °C 5 s. Remove one tube after 6, 8, 10, 12 and the last after 14 cycles [1][2]. (30 min)
  27. Run the products on an 8% non-denaturing TBE gel (180 V, about 40–50 min) and pick the lowest cycle number with a clear ~175 bp band [1][4]. (1.5 h)
  28. Excise the ~175 bp band, elute overnight, precipitate and resuspend in 15 µL; quantify and check on an Agilent High Sensitivity DNA chip [1][2]. (overnight)
  29. Sequence on an Illumina platform (original protocol used 50-cycle single-read runs) and agree read depth with your sequencing core [2]. (1–2 days)
  30. Clip adapter CTGTAGGCACCATCAAT, remove rRNA reads, align to the genome and check footprint length and reading-frame periodicity [1][2]. (1–2 days)

Pause points

  • After purifying total RNA (steps 9–10): store precipitated RNA at −20 °C or −80 °C.
  • After overnight gel extractions (steps 13, 18, 21, 28): continue next day or store the precipitated sample at −20 °C.
  • After final library QC (step 28): store libraries at −20 °C until the sequencing slot.

Expected results

  • A glassy ribosome pellet and a visible footprint band between the 26 nt and 34 nt markers [1][2].
  • A final library with a single ~175 bp peak (one ~176 bp peak in a good library); a ~145 bp band is unextended primer background [2].
  • Reads show a footprint length distribution around the marker range and reading-frame periodicity [1].

Controls

  • Primer-only lane during cDNA gel purification [2]
  • Marker lane (26 and 34 nt) beside the sample for excision [1][2]
  • Total RNA or RNA-seq from a parallel lysate aliquot for translation efficiency
  • No-RNase or untreated lysate to confirm digestion

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely causeSolution
Little or no footprint RNAOver-digestion, poor lysate, loss during precipitationCheck lysate yield; use GlycoBlue and dry pellets properly; adjust RNase I amount [1][2]
Strong ~145 bp band in libraryUnextended RT primer carried throughExcise cDNA carefully above the primer band and reduce RT primer [2]
Smear or high-molecular-weight bands in PCRToo many cycles, re-annealed partial duplexesUse fewer cycles; choose the lowest cycle number that gives a clear band [2]
Low ligation yieldIncomplete dephosphorylation, residual salt or PEGHeat-kill PNK, wash pellets and dilute before precipitation [2][4]
Footprints do not show periodicityDigestion or lysis conditions unsuitableTest buffer salts and RNase I amount; for bacteria use micrococcal nuclease instead [1][4]

Safety and waste

  • Cycloheximide and harringtonine are very toxic: handle with gloves and avoid contact, and collect as hazardous waste [1].
  • QIAzol contains phenol and guanidine, acrylamide is a neurotoxin, formamide is a reproductive toxin, SYBR Gold is mutagenic, and NaOH is corrosive: use PPE [1].
  • Ultracentrifuge: balance tubes and follow rotor limits. Dispose of cell lysate as biohazard waste.
